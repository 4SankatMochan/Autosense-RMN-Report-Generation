## 3. Executive Summary
**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive overview of Dove campaign performance for July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and weekly revenue and conversion trends. The analysis aims to inform strategic decisions for optimizing future marketing spend and maximizing ROI.
*   **Overall Performance**: Dove campaigns demonstrated strong ROAS throughout July, with the "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign leading with a ROAS of 6.6. Overall ROAS remained consistently above 5.5, peaking mid-month.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising exhibited the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) and Conversion Rate (CVR), indicating strong direct response efficiency. Offsite channels showed moderate performance, while CTV primarily contributed to awareness with minimal direct conversion.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Budget utilization was efficient across objectives, with Conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budget. The consistent week-over-week growth in conversions and revenue suggests improving campaign efficiency and stronger shopper intent over time.

## 5. Campaign Wise Analysis

### 5.1. Top Performing Campaigns by ROAS

The top 5 campaigns by Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) for July 2025 were:
*   **CMP_2025_0001 (Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch)**: ROAS 6.6
*   **CMP_2025_0002 (Dove Deodorant Awareness)**: ROAS 1.2
*   **CMP_2025_0003 (Dove Hair Therapy)**: ROAS 4.8
*   **CMP_2025_0004 (Dove Men+Care Range)**: ROAS 3.9
*   **CMP_2025_0005 (Dove Intense Repair Shampoo)**: ROAS 3.6

These campaigns collectively contributed approximately 80% of total attributed sales for the brand, highlighting the significant impact of the "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign on overall ROAS.

### 5.3. Channel Performance: CTR and CVR

#### Metric: CTR and CVR
![Chart](gs://rmn-agentic/data_science/user/12ab34cd-5678-90ef-gh12-ijkl34567890/chart_ctr_cvr_by_channel_july2025.png/0)
Image 2: CTR and CVR by Channel – July 2025

The bar chart compares Click-Through Rate (CTR) and Conversion Rate (CVR) across key media channels. Onsite ads achieved the highest engagement and conversion efficiency (CTR 3.0%, CVR 2.8%), indicating a highly engaged audience within the platform. Offsite channels showed moderate performance with a CTR of 1.8% and CVR of 1.2%. CTV, while potentially contributing to awareness, showed minimal direct conversion with a CTR of 0.7% and CVR of 0.2%. This suggests that Onsite channels are most effective for direct response, while CTV serves a different role in the marketing funnel.

### Campaigns Comparison
**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: While specific campaign objectives are not detailed, the analysis of budget utilization by objective (Awareness, Conversion, Consideration) indicates a strategic focus on conversion, which aligns with the high ROAS observed in top campaigns.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: Overall budget utilization was efficient, particularly for conversion-focused campaigns. This suggests effective budget management in driving desired outcomes.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for direct conversions, while CTV likely plays a role in brand awareness, demonstrating a diversified channel strategy.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There is a clear trade-off between reach-focused channels (like CTV with lower direct CVR) and direct-response channels (like Onsite with higher CTR/CVR).
*   **Recommendation**: Future campaigns should leverage the strengths of each channel, optimizing onsite for conversion and exploring CTV's role in upper-funnel metrics more deeply.

## 6. Recommendations

### 1. Conduct Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis
**Action**: Investigate the factors contributing to the mid-month ROAS peak (July 15) and the significant revenue surge in Weeks 3 and 4. This includes analyzing specific ad creatives, targeting adjustments, and promotional offers active during these periods.
**Objective**: To identify high-impact strategies and replicate successful tactics in future campaigns, ensuring consistent high performance.

### 2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization
**Action**: Based on the strong performance of Onsite channels and Conversion objective campaigns, consider reallocating a portion of the budget towards these high-performing areas. Implement dynamic bidding strategies that prioritize conversion events on Onsite platforms.
**Objective**: To maximize overall campaign ROAS and attributed sales by focusing resources on the most efficient channels and objectives.

### 3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements
**Action**: Develop and execute A/B tests for ad creatives and landing page experiences specifically for Onsite channels, given their high CTR and CVR. For CTV, test different messaging and call-to-actions to better understand its impact on brand recall and awareness.
**Objective**: To continuously improve engagement and conversion rates, and to better define the role and effectiveness of each channel within the overall marketing funnel.
