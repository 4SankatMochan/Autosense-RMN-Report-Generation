## 2. Executive Summary

Campaign CMP_2025_0007 for Continental, focused on a 'Consideration' objective and utilizing 'Onsite' channels with 'Sponsored Product' media, demonstrated strong financial performance. The campaign ran from 2025-04-09 to 2025-05-15, achieving an impressive Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 9.26, generating $9.26 in sales for every dollar spent. With a total attributed sales value of $24,608.19 from a total spend of $2,658.48, the campaign successfully drove 290 conversions at a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of $9.17.

Key engagement metrics include 179,899 impressions, 3,323 clicks, and a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.85%. The campaign also generated a high volume of 10,370 "Add to Cart" events, indicating significant user interest and a healthy pipeline for potential future purchases. Furthermore, 108 new-to-brand conversions highlight its effectiveness in attracting new customers. Daily trends in ad spend, impressions, and unique reach across channels provide valuable insights into performance fluctuations and channel effectiveness over the campaign duration. These insights are crucial for optimizing future marketing investments and enhancing ROI.
