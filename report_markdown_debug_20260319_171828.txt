## 3. Executive Summary

The `CMP_2025_0005` campaign for Brand `Kissan`, focused on the `Consideration` objective via `Onsite` `Sponsored Product` placements, demonstrated strong performance and significant growth during its active period.

### 3.1. Overview

The campaign, running from July 29, 2025, to September 24, 2025, aimed at driving consideration, specifically app installs. It was managed by `ADV_2025_A` and categorized as Optimization-Based.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

A key highlight is the substantial increase in monthly conversions, soaring from 64 in July 2025 to 544 in August 2025. This growth was accompanied by robust Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) values of 10.31 in July and 9.50 in August, indicating a healthy return on investment. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) remained consistent and strong, at 2.19% in July and 2.25% in August. The campaign's total ad spend was reported at $63,499.09 against a planned budget of $10,025.72, resulting in a budget utilization of 633.36%, suggesting significant scaling beyond initial planning or a revised budget not fully captured.

### 3.3. Channel/Format Performance

The `Onsite` channel, utilizing `Sponsored Product` media, proved effective in driving `Consideration`. Key performance metrics for this objective include a total ad spend of $22,965.10, generating 90,862 impressions, 1,743 clicks, and 5,630 add-to-cart events, with a CTR of 1.83% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of $1.11.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

The significant month-over-month increase in conversions and consistently high ROAS underscore the campaign's success in driving user consideration and ultimately, desired actions. The high budget utilization, while indicating substantial investment, was justified by strong performance metrics. Further optimization could focus on understanding the specific drivers behind the August surge in conversions and ensuring budget allocations align with performance potential.
