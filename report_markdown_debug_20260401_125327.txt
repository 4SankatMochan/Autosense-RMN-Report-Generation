## 2. Executive Summary

### 2.1. Overview

This report provides a comprehensive overview of the performance of Campaign CMP_2025_2158 for the brand Lifebuoy. This 'Retention' objective campaign, utilizing 'In-App Ads' on 'App' channels, ran from 2025-06-13 to 2025-07-20. The analysis reveals key performance metrics and highlights significant budget overutilization.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

The campaign significantly overspent its planned budget of $271.02, reaching an actual spend of $813.06, resulting in a spend utilization of 300.00%. Despite the overspend, the campaign generated 634,614 impressions and reached 134,820 unique users, with 915 clicks and a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 0.14% at a Cost Per Click (CPC) of $0.89.

### 2.3. Channel/Format Performance

Performance was entirely concentrated within the 'App' channel, demonstrating its effectiveness in delivering impressions and reach for this campaign. No other channels were reported for this campaign.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

The primary insight is the substantial budget overutilization (300%), indicating a critical need for tighter budget controls and more dynamic allocation strategies. Furthermore, the absence of granular daily trend data for a 'Retention' objective campaign limits in-depth temporal analysis and proactive optimization opportunities.
