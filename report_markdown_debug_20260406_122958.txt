## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report details the performance of Lifebuoy's campaign 'CMP_2025_2158', which had a 'Retention' objective and ran from June 13, 2025, to July 20, 2025. The analysis focuses on key metrics such as ad spend, impressions, reach, clicks, and associated costs, primarily within the 'App' channel.

### Overall Performance

The campaign significantly overspent its planned budget, with an actual total ad spend of 813.06 against a planned spend of 271.02, resulting in a spend utilization of approximately 300%. Performance metrics varied across the campaign duration, with impressions ranging from 1,219 to 9,588, unique reach from 211 to 2,950, and clicks from 5 to 14. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) showed a tight range of 0.50% to 0.57%, while Cost Per Click (CPC) ranged from 0.51 to 1.43. Notably, there were no video completions recorded, making CPCV not applicable and Viewed Units zero.

### Channel Format Performance

The 'App' channel was the primary focus, aggregating a total ad spend of 813.06, generating 634,614 impressions, and reaching 134,820 unique users. This resulted in 915 clicks, an average CTR of 0.50%, and an average CPC of 1.00. The absence of video completions in this channel indicates a potential gap in video content engagement or tracking.

### Optimization Insight

The substantial overspend (300% utilization) suggests either a highly successful campaign that warranted additional investment or a significant deviation from the planned budget. Further investigation into the reasons for this overspend and its corresponding return on investment, particularly regarding retention goals, is recommended. The lack of video completions also highlights an area for optimizing creative formats or tracking mechanisms if video content was part of the retention strategy.
