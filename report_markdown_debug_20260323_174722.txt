## 2. Executive Summary

The Kissan campaign ID `CMP_2025_0005`, focused on 'Consideration' with a sub-objective of 'App Installs' through 'Sponsored Product' media, has demonstrated strong performance and a positive return on investment. The campaign, which ran from 2025-07-29 to 2025-09-24, utilized both Onsite and various digital channels.

**Key Performance Highlights:**
The campaign achieved an impressive **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 9.80**, generating $28,000.00 in attributed sales value from an actual spend of $2,760.83. It garnered 149,000 impressions, 3,400 clicks, and a healthy **Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 2.00%**. Driving consideration, the campaign resulted in 400 conversions, 8,000 add-to-cart events, and 150 new-to-brand conversions, with an average **Conversion Rate of 10.60%**. The average Cost Per Click (CPC) was $0.98, and the average Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) stood at $9.40.

**Channel Performance and Trends:**
*   **Onsite Channel:** The Onsite channel recorded 286,942 impressions, 6,436 clicks, and a CTR of 2.24%. Its CPC was notably higher at $9.87. However, there is a significant discrepancy where the Onsite channel's reported ad spend ($63,499.09) is much higher than the overall campaign's total actual spend, indicating a need for reconciliation.
*   **Digital Channels (Google, Facebook, Instagram):** Across these channels, Google consistently led in both weekly ad spend and impressions, highlighting its strong reach and primary budget allocation. Facebook followed as the second-highest spender and impression driver. Instagram, while having lower overall impressions, showed a clear upward trend in reach, indicating growth. All channels exhibited relatively stable spending patterns, though a dip was observed in Week 5, likely due to an incomplete reporting period.

**Strategic Observations and Recommendations:**
The campaign effectively drives engagement and conversions, proving successful in achieving its 'Consideration' objective. However, critical discrepancies in financial reporting require immediate attention. It is recommended to:
1.  **Reconcile Budget Discrepancies:** Clarify the significant difference between the planned budget of $10,025.72 and the actual spend of $2,760.83 to understand budget utilization and potential missed opportunities.
2.  **Investigate Onsite Spend Anomaly:** Reconcile the Onsite channel's reported ad spend with the overall campaign's actual spend to ensure accurate financial oversight.
3.  **Optimize Onsite Cost Efficiency:** Analyze the high CPC of the Onsite channel ($9.87) to identify opportunities for optimizing targeting, creative, and bidding strategies to improve cost efficiency.
4.  **Strategic Budget Allocation:** Continue leveraging Google's dominance, cautiously increase investment in Instagram given its growth trend, and maintain Facebook's contribution, while continuously monitoring performance for optimal allocation.

Overall, Campaign CMP_2025_0005 is a high-performing initiative with excellent ROI, but attention to data consistency and strategic optimization of channel-specific costs will further enhance its effectiveness.
