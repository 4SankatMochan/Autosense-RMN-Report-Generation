## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of Dove's campaign performance in July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and weekly revenue and conversion trends. The top 5 campaigns by ROAS significantly contributed to total attributed sales, with the "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign leading with a ROAS of 6.6. Daily ROAS remained consistently strong throughout the month, peaking mid-month. Onsite channels demonstrated superior Click-Through Rate (CTR) and Conversion Rate (CVR), highlighting their efficiency. Budget utilization was generally high across objectives, with conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their allocated budget. Weekly performance showed a positive trajectory, culminating in peak conversions and revenue in the final week of July.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

Dove campaigns in July 2025 exhibited strong overall performance. The top 5 campaigns by ROAS included "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" (ROAS 6.6), "Dove Hair Therapy" (ROAS 4.8), "Dove Men+Care Range" (ROAS 3.9), "Dove Intense Repair Shampoo" (ROAS 3.6), and "Dove Deodorant Awareness" (ROAS 1.2). These campaigns were responsible for approximately 80% of the brand's total attributed sales. The daily ROAS trend for Dove campaigns remained robust, consistently above 5.5, and reached a peak of 7.0 around July 15, indicating efficient conversion during the mid-month period. Attributed conversions and revenue showed consistent week-over-week growth, with Week 4 achieving the highest conversions (149) and revenue ($17,600).

### 3.3. Channel Format Performance

Analysis of Click-Through Rate (CTR) and Conversion Rate (CVR) across key media channels revealed significant differences. Onsite channels demonstrated the highest engagement and conversion efficiency with a CTR of 3.0% and a CVR of 2.8%. Offsite channels performed moderately with a CTR of 1.8% and a CVR of 1.2%. Connected TV (CTV) showed the lowest direct response, with a CTR of 0.7% and a CVR of 0.2%, suggesting its primary role is in awareness rather than immediate conversion.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

Budget utilization across campaign objectives was largely efficient. Conversion campaigns utilized 90% of their planned budget, indicating effective pacing and spend towards direct sales. Awareness campaigns slightly underspent at 93% utilization, while consideration campaigns were more conservative at 88%. The consistent weekly growth in conversions and revenue, particularly the peak in Week 4, suggests that retargeting efforts and promotional offers likely contributed to stronger shopper intent and conversion efficiency later in the month.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Action**: Conduct a deeper dive into the specific factors contributing to the mid-month ROAS peak (around July 15) and the strong performance in Week 4. Analyze creative, targeting, and promotional activities during these periods.
*   **Objective**: Identify replicable strategies and optimal timing for future campaign launches and promotional pushes to maximize ROAS.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Action**: Re-evaluate budget allocation across channels and objectives based on observed CTR and CVR performance. Consider shifting more budget towards high-performing Onsite channels for conversion-focused campaigns. For CTV, explore its role in the upper funnel and potential for brand lift studies rather than direct conversion metrics.
*   **Objective**: Optimize marketing spend for maximum ROI by aligning budget with channel effectiveness and campaign objectives, ensuring efficient utilization of resources.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Action**: Implement A/B testing for creatives and landing page experiences, particularly for Onsite and Offsite channels, to further improve CTR and CVR. Focus on elements that resonate with the target audience and drive higher engagement.
*   **Objective**: Continuously enhance campaign effectiveness and user experience, leading to incremental gains in performance metrics and overall campaign success.
