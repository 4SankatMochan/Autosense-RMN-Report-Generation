# Campaign Performance Report for CMP_2025_0001 (Dove)

This report is prepared for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to provide a comprehensive overview of campaign performance across different channels. The primary objective is to understand which channels contribute most effectively to attributed sales and to identify overall daily sales patterns. The insights derived from this analysis will be crucial in informing strategic decisions related to optimizing future marketing spend, resource allocation, and campaign targeting to maximize ROI.

## 2. Executive Summary

### 2.1. Overview

This report details the performance of Campaign CMP_2025_0001 for the brand Dove, which ran from August 2, 2025, to September 6, 2025, with a primary objective of 'Consideration'. The campaign utilized the 'Onsite' channel and focused on 'Sponsored Product' media type.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

The campaign achieved 100% spend utilization, with both planned and actual spend totaling 494.49. For the 'Onsite' channel, the campaign generated 96,550 impressions, reached 33,817 unique users, and resulted in 546 clicks. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) stood at 1.07%, with a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.97. Daily performance trends indicate fluctuations in impressions and clicks, while conversions remained low and sporadic. The daily spend for the 'Onsite' channel remained constant, suggesting a fixed budget allocation.

### 2.3. Channel Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was the sole channel reported for this campaign. Its performance metrics, including impressions, unique reach, clicks, CTR, and CPC, are detailed, highlighting its role in driving consideration.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

The constant daily spend despite fluctuating engagement metrics (impressions, clicks) and low, sporadic conversions suggests that while the campaign effectively utilized its budget, there might be opportunities to optimize targeting or creative elements within the 'Onsite' channel to improve conversion rates. Further analysis into days with high impressions but low conversions could reveal specific areas for refinement.

#### 4.2.2. Campaign Analysis

The campaign successfully reached a significant audience with 96,550 impressions and 33,817 unique users through the 'Onsite' channel. The consistent daily spend highlights a fixed budgetary approach. While the campaign generated a reasonable number of clicks (546) and a CTR of 1.07%, the observed low and sporadic conversion rates suggest a gap between engagement and desired outcomes. The fluctuations in daily impressions and clicks, despite constant spend, indicate dynamic market conditions affecting visibility and user interaction.

#### 4.2.3. Overall Campaign Impact

The campaign effectively utilized its allocated budget and achieved its reach and impression goals for a 'Consideration' objective. However, the conversion performance indicates that while users are engaging with the ads, they are not consistently progressing further down the funnel. This suggests that while the campaign is successful in building awareness and initial interest, there is room for optimizing the conversion path.
