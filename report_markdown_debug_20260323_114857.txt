# Campaign Performance Report: Lifebuoy - CMP_2025_2158

## 1. Executive Summary

Campaign CMP_2025_2158 for the Lifebuoy brand ran from June 13, 2025, to July 20, 2025, with an objective of Retention (Subscription or Refill) primarily through In-App Ads on the App channel. The planned budget for the campaign was $10,656.78.

A significant discrepancy was observed between the planned budget and the actual spend, with only $271.02 recorded as actual spend. Weekly actual spend for the 'App' channel, however, showed a progressive increase from $21.40 in early June to a peak of $1,697.44 in the week of July 13, 2025, before decreasing to $271.02 by the campaign's end. This suggests either partial reporting of actual spend or substantial budget underutilization.

The campaign successfully generated impressions across multiple channels. Above-the-Line (ATL) consistently drove the highest impressions, peaking at over 2.0 million around Week 10. Digital and Out-of-Home (OOH) channels also showed increasing trends, reaching approximately 0.5 million impressions each around Week 10. All channels demonstrated a general upward trend in impressions, peaking mid-campaign (around Week 10), indicating effective reach during that period.

The campaign effectively built impression momentum, particularly through ATL. However, the lack of comprehensive KPI data for objectives like 'Consideration', combined with the significant budget underutilization, indicates a need for immediate action. Recommendations include: investigating budget discrepancy, enhancing data collection and KPI tracking, optimizing spend and channel strategy, and ensuring budget allocation aligns with conversion efficiency for retention objectives.
