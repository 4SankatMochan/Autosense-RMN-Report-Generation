## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report provides a comprehensive overview of the performance of Campaign ID CMP_2025_0011 for Ben & Jerry's, with a primary focus on understanding its contribution to attributed sales and daily sales patterns. The insights are intended for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to inform strategic decisions for optimizing future marketing spend, resource allocation, and campaign targeting to maximize ROI.

### Overall Performance

The campaign 'CMP_2025_0011' for Ben & Jerry's, with a 'Consideration' objective, demonstrated strong performance, achieving a **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 8.94**, generating **$15,019.02 in attributed sales value** from a total spend of $1,680.39. The campaign successfully utilized 100.00% of its planned budget, with a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of $9.47. Engagement and conversion metrics were favorable, including a **conversion rate of 11.00%** from 200 total conversions. The aggregated Click-Through Rate (CTR) was 1.43%, and the aggregated Cost Per Click (CPC) was $0.99. Daily CTR started at 0.90% and ended at 2.50%, while daily CPC started at $0.52 and ended at $1.43. Total impressions reached 137,188, with a total reach of 48,429. Daily ad spend remained consistent at $73.06 throughout the campaign. However, daily impressions and reach fluctuated, indicating varying levels of audience exposure.

### Channel and Format Performance

The campaign was primarily run on the 'Onsite' channel. A notable observation is the absence of data for all video-related metrics (viewed units, video plays, average watch time, video completion rate, video completions, video starts) and several messaging metrics, which were reported as zero or null. This suggests either these features were not part of the campaign, or there were tracking/data collection issues requiring further investigation.

### Optimization Insight

A remaining budget of $219.18 was also noted, despite full utilization of planned spend, indicating a potential discrepancy in budget definitions or reporting that warrants clarification. Addressing the unknowns around video and messaging metrics is crucial for a complete understanding and future strategic planning.

## 6. Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Investigate and Resolve Video & Messaging Tracking Discrepancies
*   **Insights**: All video-related metrics are zero and messaging metrics are null, implying either these features were not used or there are critical data tracking issues.
*   **Action**: Conduct an immediate audit of the campaign setup and tracking implementation for video and messaging content to ascertain if these were intended campaign components and if tracking was correctly configured.
*   **Objective**: Confirm the intended use of video and messaging content in the campaign and, if applicable, rectify any tracking errors to ensure complete and accurate data capture for all relevant metrics in future campaigns.
*   **Timeline**: Immediate (within 1–3 days)

### Recommendation 2: Deep Dive into High-Performing Campaign Elements
*   **Insights**: The campaign demonstrated a robust ROAS of 8.94 and an 11.00% conversion rate, indicating highly effective strategies in certain areas.
*   **Action**: Perform a detailed analysis of the creative assets, targeting parameters, audience segments, and ad placements that contributed most significantly to the high ROAS and conversion rate.
*   **Objective**: Identify specific successful elements, strategies, and best practices to document and replicate in future campaigns to maintain or improve performance.
*   **Timeline**: Short-term (within 1–2 weeks)

### Recommendation 3: Optimize for Consistent Daily Impression and Reach Delivery
*   **Insights**: Daily impressions and reach fluctuated significantly despite a consistent daily ad spend, potentially leading to uneven audience exposure.
*   **Action**: Analyze the factors contributing to daily fluctuations in impressions and reach, such as ad scheduling (day parting), placement strategy, and audience availability. Adjust campaign delivery settings and bidding strategies to smooth out these fluctuations.
*   **Objective**: Achieve more consistent daily impression and reach delivery across the campaign duration, ensuring stable audience exposure and engagement.
*   **Timeline**: Short-term (within 1–2 weeks) for analysis, Mid-term (within 1 month) for implementation and monitoring.

### Recommendation 4: Clarify Budget Allocation and Reporting Discrepancy
*   **Insights**: The campaign fully utilized its planned spend of $1,680.39, yet a remaining budget of $219.18 was also noted, indicating a potential inconsistency in budget reporting.
*   **Action**: Review and reconcile budget planning documentation, campaign setup, and financial reporting to understand the discrepancy between planned spend, actual spend, and any noted remaining budget.
*   **Objective**: Establish clear and consistent budget definitions and reporting mechanisms to avoid confusion and ensure accurate financial oversight for all campaigns.
*   **Timeline**: Immediate (within 1–3 days)

### Recommendation 5: A/B Test Creative and Targeting for Enhanced Engagement
*   **Insights**: While the campaign achieved a moderate CTR of 1.43% and a CPA of $9.47, there's always potential to improve engagement and cost efficiency.
*   **Action**: Develop and A/B test new creative variations (e.g., headlines, ad copy, visuals) and refine targeting parameters based on insights from the deep dive (Recommendation 2) to appeal more effectively to the target audience.
*   **Objective**: Increase the overall CTR by a defined percentage (e.g., +20%) and further optimize the Cost Per Acquisition (e.g., reduce CPA to under $9.00) in subsequent campaigns.
*   **Timeline**: Long-term (within the next campaign cycle or quarter)
