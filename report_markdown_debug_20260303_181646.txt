## 3. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of Dove's campaign performance during July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and conversion trends.

*   **Overall Performance**: The campaigns demonstrated strong performance, with the "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign (CMP_2025_0001) achieving an outstanding ROAS of 6.6. The top 5 campaigns collectively contributed approximately 80% of total attributed sales. Daily ROAS remained consistently above 5.5 throughout July, peaking at 7.0 around the 15th of the month. Weekly conversions and revenue showed positive growth, with revenue reaching $17,600 in Week 4.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising proved to be the most efficient channel, leading with a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 3.0% and a Conversion Rate (CVR) of 2.8%. Offsite channels also performed well, while Connected TV (CTV) primarily contributed to awareness with minimal direct conversion.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Budget utilization was efficient across campaign objectives, with Conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budget, Awareness campaigns 93%, and Consideration campaigns 88%. This indicates effective budget pacing and allocation.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: The high CTR and CVR for Onsite channels suggest strong mid-funnel engagement, indicating that users who interact with Onsite ads are highly qualified and likely to convert.

### 5.6. Campaigns Comparison

**Cross-Campaign Insights**

*   **Objective Alignment**: The data indicates a strong alignment between campaign objectives and outcomes, particularly for conversion-focused campaigns like "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" which achieved the highest ROAS. Budget allocation also reflects a focus on conversion, which consumed the largest portion of the budget.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: Overall budget utilization was high (around 90% across objectives), suggesting effective financial planning and execution. The slight underspend in Awareness and Consideration campaigns offers flexibility for future strategic reallocation.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for driving direct conversions, while Offsite contributes moderately. CTV's low CTR/CVR confirms its role as an awareness-building channel, which is crucial for top-of-funnel reach, even if not directly contributing to immediate sales.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There's a clear trade-off between reach/awareness (CTV) and direct conversion efficiency (Onsite). Campaigns with strong product launches (e.g., Nourishing Body Wash) can achieve exceptional ROAS, demonstrating the power of a compelling offer combined with effective targeting.
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to prioritize and optimize Onsite channels for direct conversions. Leverage CTV for broad awareness to feed the top of the funnel, understanding its indirect contribution to overall sales.

**Objective**: Maximize the impact of every dollar spent across all campaign objectives, ensuring that Awareness and Consideration efforts are adequately funded to support the conversion funnel.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

**Action**: Given the high efficiency of Onsite channels, implement continuous A/B testing for creative variations, call-to-actions, and landing page experiences specifically for Onsite and top-performing campaigns like "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch."
**Objective**: Further enhance CTR and CVR on high-performing channels and campaigns, driving incremental improvements in ROAS and overall attributed sales.
