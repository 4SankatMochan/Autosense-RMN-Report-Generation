# Campaign Performance Report

## 1. Context

This report is prepared for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to provide a comprehensive overview of campaign performance across different channels for the brand Dove during July 2025. The primary objective is to understand which channels contribute most effectively to attributed sales and to identify overall daily sales patterns. The insights derived from this analysis will be crucial in informing strategic decisions related to optimizing future marketing spend, resource allocation, and campaign targeting to maximize ROI.

Specific details regarding individual campaign IDs, media types, campaign managers, and sub-objectives for `campaign_1` and `campaign_2` as outlined in the template are not available in the provided data. The analysis focuses on aggregate performance across campaigns and channels for the specified period.

## 3. Executive Summary

**Overview**: The Dove campaigns in July 2025 demonstrated strong performance, particularly in Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and conversion efficiency. The top 5 campaigns by ROAS contributed significantly to overall attributed sales, with the "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign leading with a ROAS of 6.6. Daily ROAS remained consistently strong throughout the month, peaking mid-month. Channel analysis revealed Onsite campaigns as highly effective for direct conversions, while CTV played a role in awareness. Budget utilization was efficient across all objectives, with Conversion campaigns pacing particularly well. Weekly conversions and revenue showed consistent growth, indicating improving campaign efficiency.

*   **Overall Performance**: Dove campaigns maintained a strong ROAS, generally above 5.5, throughout July 2025, with a peak of 7.0 around July 15. The top 5 campaigns alone accounted for approximately 80% of total attributed sales. Weekly conversions and revenue showed a positive upward trend, culminating in the highest revenue in Week 4.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising channels exhibited the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) at 3.0% and Conversion Rate (CVR) at 2.8%, indicating superior direct response efficiency. Offsite channels showed moderate performance, while Connected TV (CTV) primarily contributed to awareness with lower direct conversion metrics.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Campaign spending was well-managed, with budget utilization ranging from 88% to 93% across different objectives. Conversion campaigns utilized 90% of their allocated budget effectively. The consistent ROAS and increasing weekly revenue suggest that current strategies are largely effective, with opportunities for further optimization based on daily and channel-specific trends.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: While direct conversion from CTV was minimal (CVR 0.2%), its role in contributing to awareness, as noted in the analysis, suggests its importance in the broader marketing funnel.

*   **Recommendation**: Continue to prioritize Onsite channels for conversion-focused efforts due to their high CTR and CVR. Leverage CTV strategically for brand awareness and top-of-funnel reach, understanding its indirect impact on sales.

## 6. Recommendations

Based on the analysis of Dove campaign performance in July 2025, the following recommendations are proposed to further optimize marketing spend and maximize ROI:

### 1. Conduct Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

**Action**: Deep dive into daily ROAS fluctuations and channel-specific performance to identify optimal timing and context for ad delivery. Analyze the factors contributing to the mid-month ROAS peak and consistent weekly growth.
**Objective**: Optimize ad scheduling, placement, and content delivery for maximum ROAS and conversion efficiency by aligning with peak performance periods and channel strengths.

### 2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

**Action**: Implement dynamic budget allocation strategies, prioritizing channels and objectives demonstrating higher ROAS and conversion efficiency (e.g., Onsite campaigns and Conversion objectives). Review bidding strategies to ensure they are optimized for real-time performance and market conditions.
**Objective**: Maximize overall campaign ROI by intelligently reallocating budget to best-performing areas and adjusting bidding strategies to capture high-intent audiences and opportunities.

### 3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

**Action**: Conduct systematic A/B tests on ad creatives, landing page designs, and call-to-actions, especially for Onsite and Offsite channels, to further improve CTR and CVR. For CTV, test different creative formats and messaging to enhance brand recall and awareness impact.
**Objective**: Enhance user engagement and conversion rates by identifying and implementing the most effective creative and user experience elements, thereby improving the efficiency of ad spend across all channels.
