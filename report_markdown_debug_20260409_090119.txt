## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

Campaign `CMP_2025_2158` for Lifebuoy, with a Retention objective, ran from June 13, 2025, to July 20, 2025. This report provides a detailed analysis of its performance, focusing on key metrics and daily trends to inform future marketing and sales strategies.

### Overall Performance

The campaign significantly exceeded its planned spend, utilizing 300% of the allocated budget (Total Ad Spend: 813.06 vs. Planned Spend: 271.02). Despite this, it achieved a healthy aggregated Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 7.36 and generated 114 conversions. The daily Total Ad Spend remained constant at $21.40, and daily conversions were consistently 3.0 throughout the campaign period, indicating a stable, albeit potentially over-resourced, execution.

### Channel & Format Performance

The campaign was executed entirely through the 'App' channel using 'In-App Ads'. This channel delivered the aggregated performance metrics highlighted above, including a Total Ad Spend of 813.06, 114 conversions, and a Total Campaign Revenue of 5980.86.

### Optimization Insight

The consistent daily performance for both ad spend and conversions suggests a predictable campaign rhythm. However, the substantial overspend (300% utilization) warrants further investigation to determine if the additional expenditure yielded proportional returns or if efficiency could be improved. Analyzing the Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) trend, which also remained consistent, is crucial for understanding the cost-effectiveness of each conversion.
