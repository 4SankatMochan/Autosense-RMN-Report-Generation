## 2. Executive Summary

This report details the performance of Campaign `CMP_2025_0001` for the brand Dove, which aimed for 'Consideration' with a sub-objective of 'App Installs'. The campaign, utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media on the 'Onsite' channel, had a planned spend of 494.49 but an actual spend of 164.83, resulting in a budget utilization of 33.33%.

The campaign generated a total of 88,590 impressions and 548 clicks over the analyzed period (August 2 to August 19, 2025). The average Cost Per Click (CPC) was approximately 1.09. Daily performance metrics show significant fluctuations in impressions, clicks, and CPC, indicating dynamic campaign delivery and user engagement. A notable finding is the complete absence of video engagement metrics (CPCV, viewed units), suggesting either no video assets were deployed or a critical tracking issue. These insights highlight opportunities for optimizing budget allocation, refining bidding strategies, and investigating video content integration to maximize campaign effectiveness.

### 4.2. Campaign Analysis Text

The campaign `CMP_2025_0001` for Dove, targeting 'Consideration' and 'App Installs', ran from August 2, 2025, to September 6, 2025. The actual spend of 164.83 against a planned spend of 494.49 indicates a budget utilization of 33.33% during the analyzed period.

#### 4.2.1. Daily Impressions Trend

The campaign generated a total of 88,590 impressions. Daily impressions showed considerable variability, ranging from a low of 1,339 on August 12 to a peak of 9,431 on August 18. Other days with high impressions include August 16 (9,117), August 14 (8,141), August 11 (8,078), and August 2 (8,025). This fluctuation suggests dynamic ad delivery patterns or audience availability throughout the campaign.

#### 4.2.2. Daily Clicks Trend

A total of 548 clicks were recorded for the campaign. Similar to impressions, daily clicks also varied, with the highest number of clicks occurring on August 18 (55 clicks) and August 15 (45 clicks). Days with lower click volumes included August 6 (21 clicks) and August 5 (23 clicks). Generally, higher impression days correlated with higher click volumes, indicating that increased visibility translated into user engagement.

#### 4.2.3. Daily Cost Per Click (CPC) Analysis

The average CPC for the campaign was 1.09. Daily CPC values fluctuated, with the lowest recorded CPC on August 18 (0.50) and the highest on August 16 (1.45). It is notable that August 18, which had the lowest CPC, also recorded the highest number of clicks, suggesting efficient spend on that particular day. Conversely, August 16, with the highest CPC, had fewer clicks, indicating potentially less efficient ad delivery or targeting on that day.

#### 4.2.4. Video Performance (CPCV and Viewed Units)

Despite the campaign's objective of 'Consideration' and sub-objective of 'App Installs', which might typically involve video content, the data indicates a complete absence of video engagement. For all dates in August 2025, there were no video starts, plays, quartile views, completions, or average watch time recorded. Consequently, the Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) is undefined due to zero video completions, and viewed units are reported as 0. This suggests that either video assets were not part of this specific 'Sponsored Product' campaign, or there was a critical issue with video performance tracking.

### 4.3. Overall Campaign Impact

The campaign successfully delivered a significant number of impressions and clicks, contributing to the 'Consideration' objective. However, the substantial underutilization of the budget (33.33% actual spend) and the complete lack of video engagement metrics are key areas that warrant further investigation. The daily fluctuations in performance metrics also highlight opportunities for optimizing campaign execution.
