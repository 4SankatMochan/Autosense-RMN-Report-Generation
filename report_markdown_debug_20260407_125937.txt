## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

Kissan's campaign CMP_2025_0005, primarily focused on the 'Consideration' objective with a sub-objective of 'App Installs', ran from July 29, 2025, to September 24, 2025. The campaign utilized 'Sponsored Product' media type on the 'Onsite' channel. A significant observation is the campaign's spend utilization, which reached 200% of its planned budget, indicating a substantial overspend.

### Overall Performance

The campaign exhibited fluctuating daily performance across key metrics. Impressions ranged from 2,162 to 9,856, and clicks varied from 85 to 237. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) was between 0.9% and 2.5%. Daily conversions were generally low (9 to 23), but the conversion rate itself showed a healthy range of 8% to 15%. Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) fluctuated between 7.26 and 11.75, with the lowest ROAS recorded on August 3rd.

### Channel/Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel, the sole channel for this campaign, generated an aggregated Total Ad Spend of 5521.66, 286,942 Impressions, 106,210 Reach, and 6,436 Clicks. The overall CTR for this channel was 2.24%, with a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.86. Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) was not available.

### Optimization Insight

Several anomalies were observed, including noticeable dips in impressions and clicks on specific days (e.g., August 2nd, 12th, and 18th). Conversely, some days showed a high conversion rate despite low click volumes (e.g., August 2nd and 12th), suggesting potential efficiency in specific contexts. The lowest ROAS on August 3rd also warrants further investigation. The substantial overspend (200% utilization) highlights an immediate need for budget management and optimization strategies to ensure efficient resource allocation and maximize ROI.

## 6. Recommendations

### Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Insights:** The campaign exhibited significant daily fluctuations in key performance indicators such as impressions, clicks, and ROAS. Specific days showed unusually low engagement (e.g., August 2nd, 12th, 18th) while others demonstrated high conversion rates with surprisingly low click volumes (e.g., August 2nd, 12th). The lowest ROAS was observed on August 3rd.
*   **Action:** Conduct a detailed, day-by-day analysis of campaign performance, correlating these fluctuations with external factors (e.g., promotional events, competitor activity, platform changes) and internal campaign adjustments (e.g., creative changes, bid adjustments, audience targeting modifications). Investigate the specific conditions that led to high conversion rates with low clicks to identify replicable success factors.
*   **Objective:** To understand the root causes of performance anomalies and identify specific drivers of efficient conversions.
*   **Timeline:** Immediate (within 1 week).

### Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Insights:** The campaign significantly overspent its planned budget by 200%, indicating a lack of effective spend control. This overspending may have led to diminishing returns on certain days or periods, particularly given the fluctuating ROAS.
*   **Action:** Implement a more dynamic budget allocation system that allows for real-time adjustments based on daily performance trends and ROAS. Revise bidding strategies to prioritize efficient conversions and avoid overspending on days or segments with historically low performance. Consider setting daily or weekly spend caps to prevent future budget overruns.
*   **Objective:** To improve budget efficiency, prevent overspending, and maximize overall campaign ROAS.
*   **Timeline:** Short-term (within 2-4 weeks).

### Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Insights:** The observation of high conversion rates with low clicks on certain days suggests that specific creative elements or landing page experiences might be exceptionally effective for a niche audience or under particular conditions.
*   **Action:** Design and execute systematic A/B tests for various creative assets (ad copy, visuals) and landing page designs. Focus on testing elements that were active during periods of high conversion efficiency. The goal is to isolate and scale the components that drive high-quality engagement and conversions.
*   **Objective:** To identify and optimize high-performing creative and landing page elements to consistently improve conversion rates.
*   **Timeline:** Ongoing.
