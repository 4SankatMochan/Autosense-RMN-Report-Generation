## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report provides a comprehensive overview of the 'CMP_2025_0003' campaign for the 'Lux' brand, which focused on a 'Consideration' objective with 'App Installs' as a sub-objective. The campaign ran from August 1, 2025, to September 11, 2025, with a planned and actual spend of 586.15, indicating 100% budget utilization.

### Overall Performance

For August 2025, the campaign achieved an average Click-Through Rate (CTR) of approximately 1.07%. The Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) for August 2025 was approximately 0.87, suggesting that for every dollar spent, 0.87 dollars were returned. The campaign recorded 65 total conversions in August 2025. It is important to note that ROAS and conversion analyses are based on single data points for August 2025, limiting trend identification.

### Channel/Format Performance

Under the 'Consideration' objective, the 'Onsite' channel demonstrated significant activity, with a Total Ad Spend of 5861.5 (for this specific channel and objective), generating 113,438 Impressions and reaching 37,086 Unique Users. It resulted in 632 Clicks, a CTR of 1.066%, and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.989. Notably, this channel also drove 6,901 'Add To Cart' events, indicating strong engagement towards the consideration phase.

### Optimization Insight

The campaign achieved its planned budget utilization. While the CTR is around 1%, the ROAS of 0.87 suggests that the campaign is not yet profitable based on the August 2025 data. The high number of 'Add To Cart' events (6,901) for the 'Onsite' channel under the 'Consideration' objective indicates strong user interest, but this needs to be translated into higher conversions and ultimately, a better ROAS. Further analysis with more historical data for ROAS and conversions is crucial to identify trends and optimize performance.
