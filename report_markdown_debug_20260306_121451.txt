## 3. Executive Summary

**Overview**: This report provides an analysis of the Lux brand's campaign, CMP_2025_0003, which ran from August 1 to September 11, 2025. The campaign's primary objective was Consideration, specifically targeting App Installs through the Onsite channel using Sponsored Product media. The campaign demonstrated a strong average Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and achieved a 64.49% budget utilization.

*   **Overall Performance**: Campaign CMP_2025_0003 achieved an impressive average ROAS of approximately 8.05, with an average Click-Through Rate (CTR) of about 1.07% and an average of 3.26 conversions. The campaign utilized 64.49% of its planned budget, indicating a conservative spend against the planned allocation.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Operating exclusively on the Onsite channel as a Sponsored Product, the campaign's ROAS varied significantly across different audience segments. Loyalty Program Members showed an exceptionally high ROAS of 54.10, followed by App Engaged Shoppers at 42.94, highlighting the effectiveness of targeting engaged customer groups.
*   **Optimization Insight**: The substantial difference in ROAS across audience segments suggests a significant opportunity for optimizing future budget allocation and targeting strategies. Focusing on high-performing segments can further enhance campaign efficiency and ROI.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: With a primary objective of Consideration (App Installs), the campaign successfully engaged users in the mid-funnel. The monthly conversion trends, while fluctuating, indicate consistent activity towards this objective.
