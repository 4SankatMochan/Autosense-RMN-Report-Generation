## 2. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report is prepared for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to provide a comprehensive overview of campaign performance across different channels for Lux's campaign CMP_2025_0003. The primary objective is to understand which channels contribute most effectively to attributed sales and to identify overall daily sales patterns. The insights derived from this analysis will be crucial in informing strategic decisions related to optimizing future marketing spend, resource allocation, and campaign targeting to maximize ROI.

### Overall Performance

The campaign CMP_2025_0003 for Lux, with a 'Consideration' objective, ran from August 1st to September 11th, 2025. The planned spend was $586.15, while the total ad spend reached $5861.50, resulting in a spend utilization of approximately 10.0. Daily ad spend remained constant at $30.85 throughout August 2025. The campaign consistently generated impressions, clicks, and conversions, with a generally healthy Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) ranging from 6.44 to 11.83.

### Channel Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was the primary channel for this campaign, accounting for a total ad spend of $586.15. This channel delivered 113,438 impressions, reached 37,086 unique users, and generated 632 clicks. The average Click-Through Rate (CTR) was approximately 1.07%, and the average Cost Per Click (CPC) was about $0.99.

### Optimization Insight

The consistent daily spend suggests either a fixed daily budget or a reporting anomaly where cumulative spend is reported daily. Further investigation into the actual daily incremental spend is recommended. The significant fluctuation in daily impressions and ROAS indicates opportunities for optimizing ad delivery and targeting to achieve more consistent performance. The strong ROAS values suggest that the campaign is driving sales effectively, but there's potential to enhance efficiency by analyzing the causes of daily variations.

*   **Action**: Conduct a detailed investigation into the daily spend data to confirm whether the reported "Actual Spend to Date" is incremental daily spend or a cumulative value. Analyze the specific dates with peaks and valleys in impressions and ROAS to correlate with any promotional activities, external events, or changes in ad placements.

*   **Timeline**: Immediate (within the next 1-2 weeks).

*   **Insights**: The campaign exhibits healthy ROAS values (6.44 to 11.83), but daily performance varies. Optimizing budget allocation based on peak performance times or days could enhance overall efficiency.
*   **Action**: Implement a more dynamic budget allocation strategy, potentially shifting spend to days or periods when ROAS and engagement are historically higher. Review and adjust bidding strategies to capitalize on high-performing segments identified through granular analysis.
*   **Objective**: To maximize Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and campaign efficiency by aligning spend with periods of optimal performance.
