## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report details the performance of the 'CMP_2025_2158 - Lifebuoy - App - Retention - In-App Ads' campaign, which ran from June 13, 2025, to July 20, 2025. The campaign, classified as 'Optimization-Based', utilized 'In-App Ads' on the 'App' channel with the primary objective of 'Retention' and a sub-objective of 'Subscription or Refill'. The initial planned budget for this campaign was 271.02.

### Overall Performance

The campaign's total ad spend reached 6888.08, significantly exceeding the planned budget of 271.02, resulting in a budget utilization of 2541.54%. Despite this substantial overspend, the campaign delivered an impressive Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 22.07 and generated an Incremental Sales Lift of 5980.86. This indicates a highly effective investment in driving customer retention.

### Channel/Format Performance

The 'App' channel, with a total ad spend of 271.02, was central to this campaign's success. It achieved 114 conversions and a Conversion Rate (CVR) of 12.46%. While no direct repeat transactions or units sold were recorded within the provided data for this channel, the overall campaign's high ROAS and incremental sales lift underscore the effectiveness of 'In-App Ads' in the 'App' channel for the retention objective.

### Optimization Insight

The extraordinary ROAS and Incremental Sales Lift, coupled with a solid CVR, strongly suggest that the 'In-App Ads' within the 'App' channel are a potent mechanism for achieving retention goals for the Lifebuoy brand. The significant difference between planned and total ad spend, alongside high performance metrics, warrants further investigation into the dynamic budget allocation and the specific factors that justified such an expanded investment, to replicate this success in future campaigns.
