## 2. Executive Summary

Campaign CMP_2025_0005 for Kissan, with a 'Consideration' objective focused on 'App Installs', ran from 2025-07-29 to 2025-09-24. The campaign significantly exceeded its planned spend, reaching an actual spend of 5521.66 against a planned 2760.83, indicating a high budget utilization of approximately 200%. Overall performance metrics include 286,942 impressions, 106,210 unique reach, and 6,436 clicks, resulting in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.98% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.96. Daily trends for ad spend, unique reach, and impressions show variations across channels, providing insights into channel effectiveness over time. These insights are crucial for optimizing future marketing spend and resource allocation.
