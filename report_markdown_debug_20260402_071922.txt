## 2. Executive Summary

### 2.1. Overview

This report details the performance of Campaign ID CMP_2025_0005 for the Kissan brand, which ran from July 29, 2025, to September 24, 2025. The campaign's primary objective was 'Consideration', with a sub-objective of 'App Installs', utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media type on the 'Onsite' channel. A key finding is the significant overspend, with total ad spend being double the planned budget, resulting in a spend utilization of 2.00.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

The campaign recorded a total ad spend of 5521.66 against a planned spend of 2760.83, indicating a spend utilization of 2.00. Daily aggregated metrics show consistent daily spend of approximately 250.98. Impressions varied significantly, ranging from 4,324 to 19,712, with corresponding fluctuations in unique reach. Total clicks also showed variability, from 170 to 474. The average Click-Through Rate (CTR) ranged from 0.9% to 2.5%, and the average Cost Per Click (CPC) ranged from 0.53 to 1.48.

### 2.3. Channel Format Performance

The campaign was executed exclusively on the 'Onsite' channel. Daily trend analyses for total ad spend, unique reach, and impressions reveal fluctuations over the campaign period. While daily spend remained relatively consistent, the effectiveness in terms of impressions and unique reach varied considerably, suggesting opportunities for optimization within the 'Onsite' channel.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

The substantial overspend (200% utilization) combined with the variability in daily performance metrics (impressions, reach, clicks, CTR, CPC) highlights a critical need for more rigorous budget management and performance monitoring. Identifying the specific days or periods of high efficiency versus low efficiency could inform future targeting and bidding strategies to improve overall campaign effectiveness and resource allocation.
