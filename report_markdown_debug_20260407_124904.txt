## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report provides a detailed analysis of Lifebuoy's campaign CMP_2025_2158, which focused on a Retention objective, running from June 13, 2025, to July 20, 2025. The primary channel for this campaign was 'App'.

### Overall Performance

The campaign significantly overspent its planned budget, with a spend utilization of 300.00% (Total Ad Spend: 813.06 vs. Planned Spend: 271.02). Despite this, the campaign generated a strong Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 7.36 and 114 conversions, leading to a Total Campaign Revenue of 5980.86. The Cost Per Acquisition (CVA) for the 'App' channel was 7.13.

### Channel Format Performance

The 'App' channel was the sole channel identified for this campaign, demonstrating aggregated metrics of 813.06 in Total Ad Spend, 114 Conversions, a CVA of 7.13, a ROAS of 7.36, and Total Campaign Revenue of 5980.86. No units sold were recorded for this channel.

### Optimization Insight

Daily trends show a consistent ad spend and conversion rate, with conversions holding steady at 3.0 per day. However, the CVA exhibited fluctuations, notably dipping around June 23rd, June 29th, and July 19th, while generally hovering around 12%. This suggests that while spend and conversions were stable, the cost-effectiveness of conversions varied, indicating opportunities to optimize CVA during periods of lower efficiency. The substantial overspend relative to the planned budget also highlights a need for better budget management or a reassessment of planned spend for future retention campaigns.

## 6. Recommendations

### 1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Insights**: The CVA showed noticeable dips on specific dates (June 23rd, June 29th, July 19th) despite consistent daily ad spend and conversions. This indicates periods of decreased efficiency in acquiring conversions.
*   **Action**: Conduct a deeper dive into the specific factors influencing CVA on these dates. This could include examining ad creatives, targeting parameters, competitive landscape, or external events during those periods.
*   **Objective**: Optimize campaign efficiency and reduce Cost Per Acquisition (CVA) by identifying and mitigating factors leading to performance dips.
*   **Timeline**: Immediately for retrospective analysis; ongoing for future campaign monitoring.

### 2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Insights**: The campaign significantly exceeded its planned spend (300% utilization) while maintaining a constant daily ad spend. This suggests either a poorly estimated planned spend or a missed opportunity for dynamic budget adjustments based on real-time performance.
*   **Action**: Implement a more flexible budget allocation strategy that allows for real-time adjustments based on CVA trends and conversion efficiency. Consider exploring smart bidding strategies that optimize for CVA or ROAS.
*   **Objective**: Improve budget management, ensure spend aligns with planned targets, and maximize ROAS by dynamically allocating resources to periods of higher efficiency.
*   **Timeline**: Short-term (next campaign cycle) for implementation, continuous monitoring.

### 3. Review of Planned Spend and Performance Forecasting

*   **Insights**: The substantial difference between planned spend (271.02) and total actual spend (813.06) suggests a potential mismatch in initial campaign planning or an unrecorded decision to scale the campaign.
*   **Action**: Review the process for setting planned spend and integrate performance forecasting models to better anticipate actual spend and potential ROI. Ensure clear documentation for any budget adjustments made during the campaign.
*   **Objective**: Enhance accuracy in campaign planning and forecasting, leading to more realistic budget expectations and improved strategic decision-making.
*   **Timeline**: Prior to the launch of the next campaign.
