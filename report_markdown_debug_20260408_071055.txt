## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

This report details the performance of Brooke Bond's campaign 'CMP_2025_0011', which ran from May 5, 2025, to June 10, 2025. The campaign, focused on the 'Consideration' objective, utilized an 'Onsite' channel with 'Sponsored Product' media types. The planned spend of 32.21 was fully utilized, indicating efficient budget allocation.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated consistent performance in key metrics, with total ad spend, impressions, reach, clicks, CTR, and CPC showing specific ranges. Total Ad Spend remained stable at 0.87. Impressions ranged from 1050 to 9383, and Reach from 396 to 4446. Clicks varied between 1 and 2, with a consistent CTR of 0.9%. The CPC ranged from 0.5 to 0.87. Notably, there were no video completions or viewed units recorded (CPCV and Viewed Units were 0).

### 3.3. Channel Performance

For the 'Onsite' channel, the campaign achieved a total ad spend of 32.21, generating 183,631 impressions and reaching 66,965 users. There were 40 clicks, resulting in an average CTR of 0.9% and an average CPC of 0.84. The absence of video completions meant CPCV could not be calculated for this channel.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

The 100% spend utilization is a positive indicator of budget management. However, the lack of video completions suggests that the video components, if any, were not engaged with, or the campaign did not include video elements. Further analysis of daily trends in ad spend and reach provides insights into campaign dynamics, which can inform future optimization strategies, particularly concerning creative effectiveness and media mix.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Insights:** Daily trends for ad spend and reach are available, providing a granular view of campaign activity. However, the lack of daily impression data hinders a complete understanding of daily visibility. The consistent CTR suggests stable ad performance, but daily variations in clicks and CPC could be further explored.
*   **Action:** Investigate the reason for the unavailability of daily impression data and implement measures to collect it for future campaigns. Conduct a deeper analysis of the daily ad spend and reach trends, correlating them with specific campaign events, creative changes, or external market factors to identify optimal pacing strategies.
*   **Objective:** Improve understanding of daily campaign performance drivers and optimize future media planning.
*   **Timeline:** Ongoing, with immediate focus on data collection for impressions.

### 6.2. Evaluate Video Content Strategy

*   **Insights:** The CPCV and Viewed Units metrics were consistently 0, indicating a complete absence of video completions or plays. This suggests that any video assets included in the campaign were not engaged with, or that video was not a component of this specific campaign, despite the template allowing for these metrics.
*   **Action:** Verify if video content was intended for this campaign. If so, analyze the creative, placement, and targeting to understand why no video completions or views were recorded. If video is not a core component of the 'Consideration' objective for this channel, ensure that irrelevant metrics are excluded from future reporting to avoid confusion.
*   **Objective:** Optimize media mix and content effectiveness by ensuring all campaign elements contribute meaningfully to the objective.
*   **Timeline:** Immediate review of campaign setup and creative assets.

### 6.3. Optimize Click-Through Performance

*   **Insights:** The campaign maintained a stable CTR of 0.9%, and clicks ranged from 1 to 2 daily, with CPC varying between 0.5 and 0.87. While the spend utilization was 100%, there might be opportunities to enhance user engagement and click volume within the allocated budget.
*   **Action:** Conduct A/B testing on different ad creatives, headlines, calls-to-action, and landing page experiences. Focus on elements that can improve the relevance and appeal of the ads to the target audience, aiming to increase CTR and drive more clicks.
*   **Objective:** Enhance user engagement, improve the efficiency of ad spend, and potentially drive higher consideration outcomes.
*   **Timeline:** Implement A/B testing in the next campaign cycle or during ongoing campaign optimization.
