# Campaign Performance Report - July 2025

## 1. Context
This report is prepared for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to provide a comprehensive overview of campaign performance across different channels. The primary objective is to understand which channels contribute most effectively to attributed sales and to identify overall daily sales patterns. The insights derived from this analysis will be crucial in informing strategic decisions related to optimizing future marketing spend, resource allocation, and campaign targeting to maximize ROI.

## 2. Customization Options

## 3. Executive Summary
**Overview**: This report provides an in-depth analysis of Dove campaigns conducted in July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and weekly performance trends. The primary objective was to optimize marketing spend and maximize ROI.
*   **Overall Performance**: The top 5 campaigns by ROAS contributed approximately 80% of total attributed sales, with "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" leading with a ROAS of 6.6. Daily ROAS for Dove campaigns consistently remained above 5.5 throughout July, peaking at 7.0 mid-month, indicating strong conversion efficiency.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising demonstrated superior engagement and conversion efficiency, achieving the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 3.0% and Conversion Rate (CVR) of 2.8%. In contrast, Connected TV (CTV) showed minimal direct conversion (CTR 0.7%, CVR 0.2%), suggesting its primary role is in awareness rather than direct sales.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Budget utilization was efficient across objectives, with Conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budget, Awareness campaigns 93%, and Consideration campaigns 88%. This indicates a disciplined approach to spending.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: Not explicitly detailed in the provided data.
*   **Top Performing Segments**: The "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign was the top performer. Onsite channels proved most effective for direct conversions.
*   **Underperforming Areas**: CTV, while potentially valuable for awareness, showed the lowest direct conversion efficiency, warranting a review of its role in the conversion funnel.

### 5.2 Campaign Insight
The overall performance of Dove campaigns in July 2025 was robust, with a strong ROAS trend consistently above 5.5. The "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign demonstrated exceptional efficiency, leading the top 5 campaigns that collectively drove a significant portion of attributed sales. Onsite channels proved to be highly effective in driving both clicks and conversions, indicating strong user intent and effective ad placement within the owned media environment. While CTV contributed to brand awareness, its direct conversion metrics were low, suggesting a different strategic role. Budget utilization across all objectives (Awareness, Conversion, Consideration) was managed effectively, with actual spend closely aligning with planned budgets. The consistent week-over-week growth in conversions and revenue, culminating in a peak in Week 4, highlights the increasing effectiveness and shopper intent as the campaigns progressed through the month.

## Campaigns Comparison
**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: Campaigns like "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" (CMP_2025_0001) demonstrated excellent alignment between campaign execution and the objective of driving high ROAS, indicating successful targeting and messaging for conversion.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: The overall budget utilization across objectives (93% for Awareness, 90% for Conversion, 88% for Consideration) suggests a well-managed spend, preventing significant over or under-expenditure.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for direct response and conversion, while CTV appears to serve a more upper-funnel, awareness-building role. This differentiation is critical for optimizing channel-specific strategies.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There's a clear trade-off between reach-oriented channels (like CTV) and direct-response channels (like Onsite). While CTV may not drive immediate conversions, its contribution to brand awareness could indirectly influence the strong performance seen in other channels and overall ROAS.
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to leverage Onsite channels for high-intent conversions and explore optimization strategies for CTV to either improve its direct conversion efficiency or better measure its indirect impact on the overall sales funnel.

## 6. Recommendations

### 1. Conduct Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis
**Action**: Analyze the specific factors contributing to the mid-month ROAS peak (around July 15) and the consistent growth in conversions and revenue in Week 4. This includes examining creative refreshes, promotional offers, media placements, and audience segments active during these periods.
**Objective**: To identify actionable insights and replicable strategies that can be applied to future campaigns to consistently achieve higher ROAS and sustained growth.

### 2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization
**Action**: Implement a more dynamic budget allocation model that can shift resources towards top-performing campaigns (e.g., "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch") and channels (Onsite) in real-time, especially during periods of high efficiency. Review bidding strategies for CTV to align with its awareness objective, potentially shifting from conversion-focused bids to reach or engagement-focused bids.
**Objective**: To maximize overall campaign ROAS by reallocating spend to channels and campaigns demonstrating the highest return, and to ensure each channel's budget is optimized for its specific role in the marketing funnel.

### 3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements
**Action**: Initiate systematic A/B testing for creative variations and landing page experiences, particularly for Onsite ads, given their high CTR and CVR. Focus on elements that can further enhance conversion rates and average order value.
**Objective**: To continuously improve the user journey and conversion funnel, leveraging the high engagement of Onsite channels to drive even greater efficiency and ROI.
