## 3. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive overview of Campaign CMP_2025_0001 for the brand Dove, focusing on its performance and key metrics. The campaign, with a "Consideration" objective and "App Installs" sub-objective, ran from August 2, 2025, to September 6, 2025. It was primarily executed through "Onsite" channels using "Sponsored Product" media.

The campaign's planned spend was 494.49, with an actual spend of 54.94. While the campaign overview indicated a 100% spend utilization based on a total ad spend of 494.49, a detailed KPI summary reported a total ad spend of 4697.655. Key performance indicators include 96,550 impressions, 33,817 unique reach, and 546 clicks, resulting in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.066111% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.974444. Notably, there were no video completions or viewed units recorded, leading to an uncalculable Cost Per Completed View (CPCV).

Daily analysis reveals a consistent daily ad spend of 27.47 for the Onsite channel between August 2nd and August 19th, 2025. Daily CTR fluctuated, with peaks on August 5th (1.5%), August 12th (1.72%), and August 19th (1.69%). Daily clicks also varied, reaching highs of 55 on August 18th and 45 on August 15th. The absence of video completions throughout the campaign period suggests a potential misalignment with the "Consideration" objective if video content was intended to drive engagement.
