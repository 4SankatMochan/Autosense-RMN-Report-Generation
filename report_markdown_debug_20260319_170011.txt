## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview
This report provides a comprehensive overview of the performance of Campaign ID 'CMP_2025_0005' for Brand 'Kissan'. The campaign, primarily focused on the 'Consideration' objective via 'Onsite' channels and 'Sponsored Product' media type, ran for 57 days from July 29, 2025, to September 24, 2025. The insights herein are crucial for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to optimize future marketing spend, resource allocation, and campaign targeting.

### 3.2. Overall Performance
The campaign demonstrated strong growth in conversions, significantly increasing from 64 in July 2025 to 544 in August 2025, indicating a positive trend in performance. The overall Click-Through Rate (CTR) has shown remarkable consistency at 1.0% across various observed periods, suggesting stable user engagement. The Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) has also shown healthy figures, with examples like 7.96 on August 17, 2025, and 9.99 on August 14, 2025, for specific dates. The campaign utilized approximately 27.54% of its planned budget of 10025.72, with an actual spend of 2760.83.

### 3.3. Channel and Format Performance
The campaign's primary channel is 'Onsite' using 'Sponsored Product' media type, targeting a 'Consideration' objective. For this objective and channel, the campaign achieved 286,942 impressions, reaching 106,210 unique users with a frequency of 2.88. The ROAS for this specific objective and channel was 9.61, with a CPM of 221.30. It generated 6,436 clicks, resulting in a CTR of 1.98% and a CPC of 0.96. Furthermore, the campaign led to 16,404 'Add To Cart' events and a Conversion Rate (CVR) of 10.49%, contributing to 608 conversions and a total campaign revenue of 53,048.1.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight
The significant increase in monthly conversions from July to August 2025, coupled with a healthy ROAS and CVR for the Consideration objective, suggests effective campaign execution and a strong potential for further scaling. The consistent CTR indicates a stable level of user interest in the ad format. Future optimization efforts should focus on understanding the drivers behind the conversion growth and potentially reallocating budget to capitalize on the observed positive trends.
