## 2. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive overview of Campaign ID CMP_2025_0003 for Brand Lux, which ran from August 1, 2025, to September 11, 2025, with a primary objective of "Consideration." The campaign successfully utilized 100% of its planned budget of 586.15.

Analysis of Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) reveals that "Loyalty Program Members" consistently delivered the highest ROAS, followed closely by "Frequent Browsers." Conversely, "App Engaged Shoppers" showed the lowest average monthly ROAS, indicating a potential area for optimization. Monthly ROAS performance remained relatively stable across all segments.

Click-Through Rate (CTR) performance varied, with notable spikes observed for "Frequent Browsers" around August 11th and for "Brand Interactors" around August 16th. Other audience segments maintained a consistent CTR of approximately 0.9%.

These insights suggest that while the campaign effectively engaged core valuable segments, there are opportunities to refine targeting and creative strategies for underperforming segments and to understand the drivers behind peak CTR performance.
