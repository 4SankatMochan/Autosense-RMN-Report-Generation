# Campaign Performance Report: Kissan - CMP_2025_0005

## 1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of the Kissan brand's "CMP_2025_0005" campaign, an Optimization-Based, Onsite Sponsored Product campaign focused on a 'Consideration' objective with a sub-objective of 'App Installs'. The campaign ran from July 29, 2025, to September 24, 2025.

The campaign demonstrated a significant spend utilization of 2.00, with an actual spend of $5521.66 against a planned spend of $2760.83, indicating an over-delivery or increased investment in the campaign. Daily performance metrics show considerable fluctuations across key indicators such as impressions, clicks, and conversions. Despite these daily variations, the campaign maintained a healthy Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), generally ranging between 7.26 and 11.75. The 'Onsite' channel was the primary focus, consistently showing daily ad spend of approximately $250.98. Visualizations highlight the daily trends in ad spend, impressions, and unique reach for this channel, underscoring the dynamic nature of campaign engagement.

Overall, the campaign effectively drove consideration, with consistent daily spend and a strong ROAS. The insights derived from the daily fluctuations in impressions, clicks, and conversion rates will be crucial for optimizing future marketing spend, resource allocation, and targeting strategies to maximize ROI for the Marketing and Sales leadership team.
