# Campaign Performance Report for CMP_2025_0011 (Brand Cif)

## 1. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of Campaign ID CMP_2025_0011 for the brand Cif, focusing on its performance against the 'Consideration' objective. The campaign, which ran from May 8, 2025, to May 24, 2025, utilized 'Sponsored Product' media types across the 'Onsite' channel.

The campaign's planned spend was 90.9, with actual spend matching this figure as of the latest available date. Daily ad spend remained highly consistent at approximately 5.35 throughout the campaign duration, indicating a stable budget allocation. While daily impressions and clicks varied, the campaign consistently achieved 1 conversion per day, with a consistent daily CTR of 0.9% and conversion rates between 10% and 15%. Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) fluctuated daily, ranging from a low of 6.1 to a high of 11.82, and Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) remained constant at 5.35.

Key performance indicators for the 'Onsite' channel show a total ad spend of 818.1, generating 99,782 impressions, reaching 4,451 unique users, and achieving 98 clicks, resulting in a CTR of 0.098216. The consistent daily spend and conversions, alongside varying engagement metrics, suggest a need for optimization strategies to maximize impressions and clicks within the established budget.
