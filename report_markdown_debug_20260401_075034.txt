## 2. Executive Summary

The Kissan campaign `CMP_2025_0005`, with a 'Consideration' objective and 'App Installs' sub-objective, utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media on the 'Onsite' channel, demonstrated significant activity during its duration from 2025-07-29 to 2025-09-24. A notable finding is the substantial overspend, with an actual spend of 63,499.09 against a planned budget of 2,760.83, resulting in approximately 2300% budget utilization.

Despite the budget deviation, the campaign generated 286,942 impressions, achieved a unique reach of 106,210 users, and garnered 6,436 clicks. This resulted in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.98% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.96. Daily trends for ad spend, unique reach, and impressions across the 'Onsite' channel indicate varying levels of activity and engagement throughout the campaign period. These insights are critical for understanding past performance and for informing strategic decisions to optimize future marketing spend, resource allocation, and campaign targeting to maximize ROI, particularly in achieving the 'App Installs' sub-objective.
