## 2. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive overview of the performance for Campaign ID CMP_2025_0003 for the brand Lux, which had a 'Consideration' objective. The campaign ran from August 1, 2025, to September 11, 2025, with a planned budget of 9088.36 and an actual spend of 5861.83.

The campaign's primary channel was 'Onsite', contributing to 113,438 impressions and a unique reach of 37,086. It generated 632 clicks, resulting in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.07% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.99. A significant outcome for the 'Consideration' objective was 6,901 'Add To Cart' events.

Data for 'Retention', 'Awareness', and 'Conversion' objectives was not found, limiting a complete multi-objective performance analysis. Furthermore, a detailed analysis of monthly conversions and performance trends over time was not feasible due to insufficient historical data points. However, visualizations for monthly CTR and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) trends are available, indicating how these metrics evolved across channels over the campaign period.

The budget utilization for the campaign was approximately 64.49% of the planned budget.
