## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report analyzes the performance of Campaign CMP_2025_0001 for the brand Dove, which had a 'Consideration' objective with a sub-objective of 'App Installs'. The campaign ran from August 2, 2025, to September 6, 2025, primarily utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media type on the 'Onsite' channel. The report aims to provide key performance insights to the Marketing and Sales leadership team to inform future strategic decisions.

### Overall Performance

Campaign CMP_2025_0001 successfully utilized its entire planned spend of $494.49, achieving 100% spend utilization. The daily ad spend remained consistent at approximately $27.47 throughout the observed period. The campaign generated significant impressions (ranging from 1,339 to 9,431 daily) and reach (334 to 3,876 daily), indicating effective exposure to the target audience. Clicks varied between 19 and 55 daily, with a Click-Through Rate (CTR) ranging from 0.9% to 1.72%, and Cost Per Click (CPC) from $0.50 to $1.45. Notably, no video completions (Viewed Units) were recorded, resulting in an undefined Cost Per Completed View (CPCV).

### Channel/Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was the primary focus for this campaign. Aggregated data for this channel shows a total ad spend of $494.49, generating 96,550 impressions, reaching 33,817 unique users, and resulting in 546 clicks. The overall CTR for the 'Onsite' channel was 0.5655%, with an average CPC of $0.9057. The consistent daily spend suggests a steady investment strategy within this channel.

### Optimization Insight

The consistent daily spend and full budget utilization indicate a well-managed campaign from a financial perspective. However, the variability in daily impressions, reach, and clicks suggests opportunities for optimizing ad delivery and creative effectiveness. The absence of 'Viewed Units' and 'CPCV' warrants further investigation, especially if video content was intended to be part of the 'Sponsored Product' media type, or if 'App Installs' could be better driven through video engagement. Improving CTR and managing CPC effectively will be key to enhancing the efficiency of 'Consideration' campaigns like this one.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Optimize Creative and Targeting for Engagement

*   **Insights:** While the campaign delivered consistent spend and good reach, the CTR and CPC varied, indicating potential for efficiency gains. The 'Consideration' objective with 'App Installs' sub-objective relies heavily on compelling ad experiences.
*   **Action:** Conduct A/B testing on different ad creatives, headlines, and calls-to-action within the 'Sponsored Product' format on the 'Onsite' channel. Refine targeting parameters to ensure ads are shown to the most receptive audience segments, aiming to improve CTR and reduce CPC.
*   **Objective:** Enhance campaign efficiency by driving higher quality clicks at a lower cost, thereby improving the consideration phase for app installs.
*   **Timeline:** Ongoing, with weekly analysis and iteration.

### 6.2. Investigate and Address 'Viewed Units' Anomaly

*   **Insights:** The absence of 'Viewed Units' and an undefined 'CPCV' is a critical data gap. If video content was intended to be part of the 'Sponsored Product' ads, this indicates either a tracking error or that the video content is not being consumed.
*   **Action:** Verify the implementation of video completion tracking for 'Sponsored Product' ads. If tracking is correctly set up, analyze the video content itself for engagement factors (e.g., length, messaging, placement). Consider introducing or optimizing video assets to improve user consideration and drive app installs.
*   **Objective:** Ensure accurate measurement of video engagement and leverage video content effectively to support the 'Consideration' objective and 'App Installs' sub-objective.
*   **Timeline:** Immediate investigation, with implementation of changes within the next campaign iteration.

### 6.3. Leverage Daily Performance Trends for Dynamic Allocation

*   **Insights:** The daily trends for impressions and reach show fluctuations. Understanding the patterns behind these variations can enable more strategic budget allocation.
*   **Action:** Analyze the daily performance data (impressions, reach, clicks) in conjunction with contextual factors such as day of the week, time of day, and any observed market trends or competitive activities. Use these insights to dynamically adjust daily budget allocation or ad scheduling to maximize exposure and engagement during peak performance periods.
*   **Objective:** Maximize campaign impact by optimizing ad delivery during periods of highest audience receptiveness and cost-effectiveness.
*   **Timeline:** Weekly review and adjustment of budget and scheduling.
