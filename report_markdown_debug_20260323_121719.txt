## 2. Executive Summary

This summary outlines the performance and key insights for Campaign ID 'CMP_2025_2158' for the brand 'Lifebuoy', an In-App Ads retention campaign that ran from June 13, 2025, to July 20, 2025, with a sub-objective of Subscription or Refill.

### 2.1. Overview

The campaign demonstrated a clear focus on retention through In-App Ads. While an initial planned budget of 10,656.78 was noted, the reported segment indicated a planned budget and actual spend of 271.02, which was 100% utilized. Analysis of overall weekly ad spend showed a consistent and significant upward trend from November 2023 to October 2024, indicating expanding investment.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

The campaign's success is heavily reliant on Google for driving both spend and conversions towards its retention objective. Weekly impressions fluctuated during the campaign, peaking around the second week of July 2025 with 130,758 impressions, and significantly declining to 23,946 impressions in the last week coinciding with the campaign end.

### 2.3. Channel Performance

*   **Google** emerged as the primary driver for both ad spend and conversions, showing consistent high performance and increasing trends.
*   **Facebook** served as a significant secondary contributor, maintaining moderate spend and conversions with steady growth.
*   **Email** recorded the lowest spend and conversions, consistent with its supplementary role.

All channels exhibited a general upward trend in both spend and conversions over the observed weeks, suggesting positive momentum.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

To further enhance performance, it is recommended to address budget discrepancies, optimize Google's effectiveness, enhance Facebook's contribution, re-evaluate Email's strategic role, and optimize impression management throughout the campaign lifecycle.

## 5. Recommendations

### 5.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Action:** Clarify and reconcile the discrepancy between the initial planned budget (10,656.78) and the reported planned budget/actual spend (271.02). Implement a standardized budget tracking and reporting mechanism that clearly distinguishes between overall campaign budget and phased/segment-specific allocated budgets to avoid misinterpretation and ensure accurate budget utilization analysis. For future reporting, ensure that the 'Planned Budget' in the high-level summary accurately reflects the segment being reported, or clearly state it as a segment of a larger overall budget.
*   **Objective:** Improve financial clarity and reporting accuracy.

### 5.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Action:** Given Google's consistent high performance in both spend and conversions, allocate additional budget to Google to further capitalize on its effectiveness. Conduct A/B testing on ad creatives, targeting parameters, and bidding strategies within Google to identify even more efficient conversion paths for retention. Explore scaling successful Google campaigns into similar audience segments. Analyze the specific Google ad formats and targeting methods that are driving the most retention-focused conversions (Subscription or Refill) and prioritize investment in those areas.
*   **Objective:** Maximize retention conversions through optimized channel investment.

### 5.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Action:** While Facebook shows moderate performance, explore opportunities to increase its conversion efficiency. Test new ad formats, lookalike audiences based on existing loyal customers, and retargeting campaigns specifically designed for retention. Analyze Facebook's creative performance to identify what resonates best with the retention objective. Focus on refining Facebook ad creatives and messaging to strongly emphasize the 'Subscription or Refill' benefits, potentially including exclusive offers for existing customers.
*   **Objective:** Enhance Facebook channel contribution to retention.

### 5.4. Re-evaluate Email Channel Role

*   **Action:** Given Email's lowest spend and conversions, re-evaluate its strategic role. If it's intended as a primary retention driver, invest in refining email segmentation, personalization, and call-to-actions to boost conversion rates. If it's a supplementary channel, ensure its content aligns with and supports other channels' messaging for a cohesive retention strategy, focusing on cost-efficiency rather than high volume. Implement advanced email segmentation based on user behavior related to 'Subscription or Refill' (e.g., users nearing subscription end, users who previously purchased but haven't subscribed). A/B test subject lines and email content for higher open and click-through rates.
*   **Objective:** Optimize the effectiveness and strategic alignment of the Email channel.

### 5.5. Optimize Impression Management

*   **Action:** Analyze the factors contributing to the peak impressions around the second week of July and the significant dip in the last week. For future campaigns, adjust ad spend and creative refreshes to potentially smooth out impression delivery, preventing sharp declines towards the campaign end, especially if continued engagement is desired beyond the official end date. Consider front-loading some budget for initial ramp-up and maintaining a steady budget throughout the campaign lifecycle. For future campaigns with similar start/end dates, plan for a gradual wind-down of impressions instead of an abrupt drop-off to maintain brand presence and facilitate continued retention efforts post-campaign.
*   **Objective:** Ensure consistent brand presence and engagement throughout the campaign lifecycle.
