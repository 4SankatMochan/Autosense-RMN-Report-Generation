## 3. Executive Summary

Campaign CMP_2025_0003 for the brand Lux, with a primary objective of "Consideration" and a sub-objective of "App Installs", ran from August 1st to August 19th, 2025, within its planned duration of August 1st to September 11th, 2025. The campaign utilized a planned spend of $586.15, which was fully utilized.

The campaign generated 113,438 impressions and achieved a unique reach of 37,086. It recorded 632 clicks, resulting in an overall Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 0.56% and an average Cost Per Click (CPC) of $0.93. The Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) could not be calculated, indicating no video completions were recorded.

From August 1st to August 19th, 2025, the campaign accumulated a total spend of $586.15, generating $216.26 in attributed sales, leading to a Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 7.01 on the last recorded day. It drove 3 conversions with a conversion rate (CVR) of 11.11% and a Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of $10.28. The campaign's daily spend was approximately $30.85, with the budget fully expended by August 19th, 2025.

Visualizations indicate trends in weekly ad spend and impressions across channels, providing deeper insights into performance fluctuations. These findings suggest that while the campaign successfully drove engagement and conversions, further analysis into video content performance (CPCV) and channel-specific optimizations could enhance future campaign effectiveness.
