**Executive Summary: Campaign CMP_2025_2158 for Brand Lifebuoy**

This summary outlines the performance and key observations for Campaign ID CMP_2025_2158, an Optimization-Based campaign for the Lifebuoy brand focused on **Retention** with a sub-objective of Subscription or Refill, executed via **In-App Ads** on the **App** channel from June 13, 2025, to July 20, 2025.

**Key Financials and Discrepancies:**
The campaign had an initial planned budget of $271.02, matching the "Actual Spend" reported for the latest date. However, the "Total Ad Spend" for the campaign was significantly higher at $15,854.67, indicating a substantial discrepancy or a different scope for the initial planned budget. This resulted in a budget utilization of 58.50% when measured against the total ad spend. Further investigation is recommended to reconcile this difference, improve budget transparency, and ensure accurate financial planning.

**Performance Trends (App Channel):**
The campaign demonstrated a structured execution with consistent activity in both spending and impression generation during its core period. Weekly ad spend on the App channel showed an initial phase ($42.79), a sustained period of $149.77 for five consecutive weeks, and a decline towards the end ($21.40). Similarly, impressions peaked mid-campaign (e.g., 43,586 in the week of July 6, 2025), aligning with the sustained spending, indicating effective reach during the campaign's active phase. This consistent flighting strategy for in-app ads proved effective in maintaining reach.

**Objective Achievement and Data Gaps:**
While the campaign aimed for 'Retention' with a focus on 'Subscription or Refill,' direct performance metrics to evaluate this objective (e.g., renewal rates, refill frequency) were not available. Additionally, data for the 'Consideration' objective and specific cost-efficiency metrics like 'Cost Per View (cp_cpv)' were not tracked, limiting a comprehensive assessment of the campaign's overall effectiveness across the customer journey.

**Recommendations:**
1.  **Financial Reconciliation:** Immediately investigate the significant budget discrepancy between planned and total ad spend to understand its root cause and refine future budgeting processes.
2.  **Enhanced Metric Tracking:** Implement robust tracking for retention-specific Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as subscription renewals and refill rates to accurately measure the campaign's primary objective.
3.  **Comprehensive Data Capture:** Ensure future campaigns track all relevant objectives (e.g., Consideration) and integrate essential cost-efficiency metrics like 'cp_cpv' for a holistic performance evaluation.
4.  **Optimize Flighting Strategy:** Continue leveraging the observed effective structured flighting strategy for ad spend and impressions, further analyzing its correlation with engagement for future refinements.

*   **Objective:** Enhance the accuracy of financial planning, improve budget transparency, and ensure that planned budgets reflect the true scope and potential evolution of campaign spending.
*   **Timeline:** Immediate (within 1-2 weeks) for reconciliation; Short-term (next quarter) for process review and implementation of revised budgeting protocols.

*   **Objective:** Accurately measure the effectiveness of the campaign in achieving its core retention objectives and provide quantifiable results for future optimization.
*   **Timeline:** Short-term (within 1 month) to define and set up tracking; Ongoing for continuous monitoring and reporting.

*   **Insight:** The analysis noted missing data for the 'Consideration' objective and the 'cp_cpv' (Cost Per View) metric, limiting a holistic evaluation of the campaign's performance across different stages of the customer journey and cost efficiency.
*   **Action:** For future campaigns, ensure that all relevant campaign objectives (e.g., Consideration) are explicitly defined and their associated performance metrics are tracked from the outset. *   **Objective:** Enable a more comprehensive assessment of campaign effectiveness, providing insights into various stages of the customer funnel and granular cost efficiencies.
