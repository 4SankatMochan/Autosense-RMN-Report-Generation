## 2. Executive Summary

### Overview

Campaign 'CMP_2025_0001' for Dove, with a 'Consideration' objective and 'App Installs' sub-objective, ran from August 2nd, 2025, to September 6th, 2025. The campaign utilized a 'Sponsored Product' media type within the 'Onsite' channel. The planned spend of $494.49 was fully utilized, indicating 100% budget execution.

### Overall Performance

The campaign generated significant engagement and positive returns. It achieved 96,550 impressions and reached 33,817 unique users, leading to 546 clicks and an average Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.07%. With 56 total conversions and an average conversion rate of 10.52%, the campaign resulted in a total attributed sales value of $4,332.56. The Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) was a strong 8.76, with an average Cost Per Click (CPC) of $0.97 and an average Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) of $10.25. Additionally, there were 4,644 add-to-cart events and 22 new-to-brand conversions.

### Channel and Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was the sole channel recorded for this campaign, accounting for the entire total ad spend and all impressions. Daily ad spend remained consistent at $27.47 from August 2nd to August 19th, 2025. Daily impressions varied, peaking at 9,431 on August 18th, 2025. Unique reach trends over the campaign period are visualized, showing daily fluctuations across the 'Onsite' channel. No video engagement was recorded for this campaign.

### Optimization Insight

The consistent daily ad spend suggests a stable budget allocation strategy. The strong ROAS and conversion metrics indicate effective targeting and creative for the 'Consideration' objective. Monitoring daily trends in unique reach and impressions is crucial for understanding audience engagement patterns and optimizing ad delivery schedules.
