## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report details the performance of Campaign ID 'CMP_2025_0011' for Ben & Jerry's, which ran from June 12, 2025, to July 04, 2025, with a primary objective of 'Consideration'. The campaign focused on 'Sponsored Product' media type through 'Onsite' channels.

### Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated strong overall performance, achieving a total ad spend of $1,680.39 with approximately 100% spend utilization against its immediate planned spend. Key performance indicators include 98,838 impressions, 1,306 clicks, and a healthy Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.39%. The campaign successfully drove 139 conversions and generated an attributed sales value of $12,052.62, resulting in an impressive Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 9.09. The Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) was efficient at $9.74, and the conversion rate stood at 10.89%. Furthermore, the campaign contributed significantly to brand growth with 4,706 add-to-cart events and 52 new-to-brand conversions.

### Channel Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was the sole channel utilized for this campaign, delivering all aggregated metrics. It accounted for the entire ad spend, impressions, reach, and clicks, with a consistent average CTR of 1.43% and CPC of $0.99.

### Optimization Insight

The high ROAS and efficient CPA indicate that the campaign was highly effective in driving sales and new customer acquisition. The daily trends in reach, impressions, and ad spend provide opportunities for granular optimization, such as adjusting spend to align with peak engagement periods or reallocating budget based on performance fluctuations.
