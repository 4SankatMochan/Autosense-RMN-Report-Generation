## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report provides a comprehensive overview of Campaign ID 'CMP_2025_0011' for Brand 'Axe', with a primary objective of 'Consideration'. The campaign ran from August 4, 2025, to September 1, 2025, with a planned and actual spend of $2335.47. Overall, the campaign achieved a budget utilization of approximately 53.12% against a larger planned spend of $37,367.52 for the overall campaign objective. Key performance indicators show varied performance across channels and weekly fluctuations in spend and impressions.

### Overall Performance

The campaign experienced its highest spend and attributed sales in the second week (starting 2025-08-10), followed by a significant decrease in the third week. Despite this reduction, the conversion rate showed a positive upward trend throughout the campaign, reaching its highest at 12.67% in the final week. The average Click-Through Rate (CTR) was approximately 2.03%, Average Conversion Rate 11.26%, Average Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) 8.87, Average Cost Per Click (CPC) $1.10, and Average Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) $10.10.

### Channel Format Performance

Among the analyzed channels, Facebook demonstrated the highest total ad spend and impressions, followed by Google and Instagram. Instagram showed the highest CTR (0.000133333), while Google and Facebook had comparable CTRs. Twitter had the lowest ad spend, impressions, and CTR, along with the highest CPC and CPCV, suggesting lower efficiency for this campaign's objective.

### Optimization Insight

A notable anomaly was the significant drop in total spend and attributed sales in the third week. However, the continued improvement in conversion rate during this period suggests that the remaining campaign efforts were highly efficient in converting users who interacted with the ads. This indicates potential for optimizing spend allocation towards higher-performing segments or channels, especially if the reduction was due to budget constraints or campaign end.
