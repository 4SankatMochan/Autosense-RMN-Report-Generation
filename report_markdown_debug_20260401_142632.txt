## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

Campaign CMP_2025_0001 for the brand Dove, with a primary objective of Consideration and a sub-objective of App Installs, ran from August 2nd to September 6th, 2025. The campaign utilized Sponsored Product media types across Onsite channels, with a planned and actual spend of $494.49.

### Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated consistent daily spend throughout its active period (August 2nd to August 19th, 2025, for detailed metrics). Impressions showed a consistent upward trend, indicating growing reach. Clicks and conversions varied daily, with CTR mostly around 0.9% and conversion rates between 8% and 15%. Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) ranged from 6.24 to 11.45, while Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) was between $5.49 and $13.74. Notably, all video-related metrics (starts, plays, quartile views, and completions) were consistently zero.

### Channel/Format Performance

For impressions, Google was the leading channel, consistently generating the highest volume, followed by Facebook and Instagram, all showing an increasing trend. The Onsite channel experienced fluctuations in daily unique reach, with notable peaks suggesting periods of higher audience engagement. Daily performance metrics by channel included Total Ad Spend, Impressions, Unique Reach, Clicks, CTR, CPC, and Video Completions.

### Optimization Insight

The consistent daily spend and increasing impressions across channels are positive indicators of reach. However, the absence of any video completions suggests that either the video content was not effectively presented, or the tracking for video metrics was not fully operational. The variability in CTR, conversion rates, ROAS, and CPA highlights opportunities for optimizing ad creatives, targeting, and bidding strategies to improve efficiency and drive higher quality engagement.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

**Action:** Conduct a deeper dive into the daily fluctuations of CTR, conversion rates, ROAS, and CPA to identify specific days or contexts where performance was exceptionally high or low. Analyze these periods against external factors (e.g., promotions, competitor activity, news events) and internal factors (e.g., creative changes, landing page updates).
**Objective:** To uncover specific drivers of performance variations and inform more precise targeting and scheduling strategies.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

**Action:** Given the observed variability in key performance indicators and the consistent daily spend, explore implementing dynamic budget allocation models that shift spend towards periods or channels demonstrating higher ROAS and conversion rates. Review and optimize bidding strategies to ensure cost-efficiency for clicks and acquisitions, especially for the Consideration objective.
**Objective:** To maximize campaign efficiency and improve the return on ad spend by intelligently reallocating resources based on real-time performance.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

**Action:** Initiate a structured A/B testing program for ad creatives, headlines, call-to-actions, and landing page content. Focus on elements that influence CTR, conversion rate, and dwell time. For the 'Onsite' channel, specifically test different product presentation formats or messaging to capitalize on the high 'Add to Cart' events.
**Objective:** To continuously improve user engagement, click-through rates, and conversion performance by identifying the most effective creative and landing page elements.

### 6.4. Investigation and Rectification of Video Metric Tracking

**Action:** Immediately investigate why all video metrics (starts, plays, quartile views, and completions) were recorded as zero. This could be a tracking implementation issue or indicate that video assets were not served or engaged with. If video was intended to be part of the campaign, ensure proper tracking is in place for future campaigns.
**Objective:** To gain complete visibility into the performance of video assets and accurately calculate metrics like CPCV, ensuring all campaign components are measurable.
