#### 3.2.2. Monthly Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) Analysis

The monthly ROAS for Campaign ID `CMP_2025_0005` for the brand `Kissan` provides insights into the campaign's profitability.

*   **Performance Trends Over Time (Monthly ROAS):**
    The monthly ROAS for the campaign showed some fluctuation but generally maintained a level around 3.0. It started at 3.03 in January, slightly decreased to 2.98 in February, recovered to 3.03 in March, dipped to 3.00 in April, and finally reached its highest point of 3.16 in May. This indicates a relatively stable performance with a positive trend towards the end of the observed period.

*   **Performance Across Different Channels:**
    When comparing ROAS across various channels, the 'Display' channel demonstrated the strongest performance with an ROAS of 3.27. The 'Search' channel followed with an ROAS of 3.03, while 'Social Media' had the lowest ROAS at 2.99. This suggests that the 'Display' channel is currently the most efficient in terms of return on ad spend for this campaign.

*   **Performance Across Audience Segments:**
    Regarding audience segments, the 'Parents' segment yielded the highest ROAS at 3.27. The 'Adults' segment performed with an ROAS of 3.03, and the 'Youth' segment had the lowest ROAS at 2.99. These results indicate that targeting the 'Parents' audience segment is currently providing the best return for the advertising investment.
