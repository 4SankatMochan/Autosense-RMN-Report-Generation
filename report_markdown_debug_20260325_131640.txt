# Campaign Performance Report: Lifebuoy - CMP_2025_2158

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Lifebuoy CMP_2025_2158 Retention Campaign**, an optimization-based initiative focused on "Subscription or Refill" via In-App Ads on the 'App' channel, ran from June 13 to July 20, 2025. The campaign demonstrated precise budget management, fully utilizing its planned budget of $271.02 with an actual spend of $271.02.

Ad spend on the 'App' channel showed a strategic pattern: an initial spend of $42.79, followed by a sustained high activity with approximately $149.77 per week for five consecutive weeks, and a decrease to $21.40 towards the campaign's conclusion. Correspondingly, 'App' channel impressions exhibited a strong, generally upward trend after the initial week, peaking at 144,369 in Week 28 (July 7-13), confirming 'App' as the best-performing channel for impressions.

Overall, the campaign successfully executed its retention objective by effectively deploying budget and generating significant impressions in the 'App' channel. Future strategic recommendations include enhancing tracking for direct retention metrics (e.g., renewal/refill rates), analyzing the impression-to-retention funnel within the app, optimizing budget allocation by concentrating spend during peak impression periods, and A/B testing in-app ad creatives to improve conversion rates.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Enhance Tracking for Direct Retention Metrics

*   **Insight from Outputs:** The campaign successfully utilized 100% of its planned budget and focused exclusively on a 'Retention' objective with a 'Subscription or Refill' sub-objective, generating strong impressions in the 'App' channel. However, the analysis does not provide specific performance metrics (e.g., renewal rates, refill rates, or direct conversions) tied to these retention objectives, making it challenging to quantify the campaign's ultimate success beyond budget utilization and impressions.
*   **Action:** Implement comprehensive tracking mechanisms to measure key retention performance indicators (KPIs) such as subscription renewal rates, refill completion rates, customer lifetime value (CLTV) for retained users, and reduction in churn directly attributable to users exposed to this campaign. Ensure these metrics are linked to the specific campaign ID and creative variations.
*   **Objective:** To gain a clear, quantitative understanding of the campaign's direct impact on its 'Subscription or Refill' objective, enabling precise ROI calculation and data-driven optimization for future retention campaigns.

### 6.2. Analyze Impression-to-Retention Funnel in the App Channel

*   **Insight from Outputs:** The 'App' channel was the best performing in terms of impressions, peaking at 144,369 in Week 28. This indicates high visibility for the in-app ads. However, there is no data to show how effectively these impressions translated into actual 'Subscription or Refill' actions.
*   **Action:** Conduct a detailed funnel analysis within the 'App' channel, tracking user behavior from impression exposure to the final 'Subscription or Refill' conversion. Identify any significant drop-off points, user friction, or abandonment during this journey. Investigate if the ad's call-to-action (CTA) or the subsequent landing experience within the app is fully optimized for retention conversions.
*   **Objective:** To optimize the in-app user journey and creative elements to maximize the conversion rate from ad impressions to actual subscription renewals or refills, thereby improving the efficiency of the high impression volume generated.

### 6.3. Strategic Budget Allocation Based on Impression Trends

*   **Insight from Outputs:** The campaign's ad spend showed a consistent, high allocation of approximately $149.77 for five consecutive weeks (from 2025-06-15 to 2025-07-13), which correlated with the highest impression volumes, peaking in Week 28. The initial and concluding weeks had significantly lower spend ($42.79 and $21.40, respectively).
*   **Action:** For future retention campaigns, analyze the specific period during which user engagement and impression delivery are historically highest (as indicated by Week 28's peak). Consider strategically front-loading or maintaining a higher budget allocation during these peak performance windows to capitalize on optimal user receptivity, rather than an even or ramp-down distribution that might miss peak opportunities.
*   **Objective:** To optimize future budget allocation by concentrating spend during periods identified for maximum user engagement and impression delivery, aiming to improve the overall cost-effectiveness and impact of retention efforts.

### 6.4. A/B Test In-App Ad Creatives and Messaging

*   **Insight from Outputs:** The 'App' channel successfully delivered a high volume of impressions, indicating the ads are being seen. However, the analysis does not provide any insights into the engagement quality of these impressions or the effectiveness of the ad creatives and messaging in driving the specific 'Subscription or Refill' sub-objective.
*   **Action:** Develop multiple versions of in-app ad creatives, including variations in messaging, visuals, and calls-to-action (CTAs) that explicitly address subscription renewal or refill benefits. Implement A/B testing to determine which creative elements resonate most with the target audience, leading to higher click-through rates (CTR) and, more importantly, higher conversion rates for the retention objective.
*   **Objective:** To enhance the persuasive power and effectiveness of in-app ad creatives to directly encourage users to renew their subscriptions or complete refills, thereby improving the campaign's ability to achieve its retention goals.
