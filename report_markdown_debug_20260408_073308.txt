## 3. Executive Summary

This report details the performance of Campaign CMP_2025_0011 for Brooke Bond, which ran from May 5, 2025, to June 10, 2025, with a primary objective of 'Consideration' focusing on 'Engagement / Clicks'. The campaign utilized a 'Sponsored Product' media type within the 'Onsite' channel. With a planned spend of $32.21, the campaign achieved 100% spend utilization, indicating efficient budget management.

Key performance indicators showed a consistent daily ad spend of approximately $0.87. Impressions ranged from 1,050 to 9,383, and reach varied from 396 to 4,446, demonstrating fluctuating but significant audience exposure. Clicks were observed in a narrow range of 1 to 2 daily, resulting in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 0.9% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) between $0.5 and $0.87. The aggregated performance for the 'Onsite' channel recorded 183,631 impressions, 66,965 reach, and 40 clicks, with a CTR of 0.0218% and CPC of $0.81. CPCV and Viewed Units were not available or zero, suggesting a focus away from video completions or specific unit views for this campaign.

Overall, the campaign successfully executed its planned spend and generated considerable impressions and reach. The daily trends for ad spend, impressions, and reach show variations, with impressions peaking around May 11th and 17th. These insights can inform future campaign strategies for optimizing engagement and managing spend.
