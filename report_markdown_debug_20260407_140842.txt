This report details the performance of Campaign ID: CMP_2025_0003 for the brand Seventh Generation, focusing on its 'Consideration' objective. ## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Insights:** The daily ad spend was constant, but impressions and reach fluctuated. This suggests that the fixed daily budget might not be optimally aligned with periods of higher audience availability or engagement.
*   **Action:** Conduct a deeper analysis of daily impressions and reach trends against external factors (e.g., day of week, seasonal events, competitor activity) to identify optimal times for ad delivery.
*   **Objective:** Maximize reach and impressions efficiency by aligning ad delivery with peak audience engagement periods.
*   **Timeline:** Immediately, for future campaign planning.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Insights:** With a fixed daily spend, the campaign might be missing opportunities to scale up during high-performing periods or scale down during low-performing periods, thereby optimizing overall campaign efficiency.
*   **Action:** Implement a dynamic budget allocation strategy that allows for adjustments based on real-time performance data (e.g., higher spend on days with better reach/impressions/CTR). Review bidding strategies to ensure cost-effectiveness, especially given the CPC range.
*   **Objective:** Improve campaign efficiency and maximize the impact of ad spend for the 'Consideration' objective.
*   **Timeline:** Next campaign cycle.

### 6.3. Investigate CPCV for 'Onsite' Channel

*   **Insights:** The CPCV for the 'Onsite' channel is marked as 'Not available (null)', indicating no video completions were recorded. This is relevant if video content was part of the 'Consideration' strategy.
*   **Action:** Verify if video assets were intended for the 'Onsite' channel and, if so, investigate the technical reasons for the lack of CPCV data. Ensure proper tracking is in place for video completion metrics.
*   **Objective:** Gain a complete understanding of video performance and ensure all relevant metrics are being tracked for comprehensive campaign evaluation.
*   **Timeline:** Within the next reporting period.
