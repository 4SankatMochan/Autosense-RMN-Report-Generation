## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

This report details the performance of Dove brand campaigns during July 2025, providing insights into campaign effectiveness, media channel performance, and budget utilization. The analysis highlights key areas of strength and opportunities for optimization to maximize ROI for future marketing efforts.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

The top 5 campaigns by Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) for July 2025 were led by "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" with an impressive ROAS of 6.6, followed by "Dove Hair Therapy" (4.8), "Dove Men+Care Range" (3.9), "Dove Intense Repair Shampoo" (3.6), and "Dove Deodorant Awareness" (1.2). These campaigns collectively contributed approximately 80% of the total attributed sales for the brand. The daily ROAS trend for Dove campaigns remained strong throughout July 2025, consistently above 5.5 and peaking at 7.0 around July 15, indicating robust conversion efficiency. Weekly attributed conversions and revenue showed a positive trajectory, with both peaking in Week 4, demonstrating improving campaign efficiency and stronger shopper intent over the month.

### 3.3. Channel Performance

Onsite advertising channels demonstrated superior performance, achieving the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 3.0% and Conversion Rate (CVR) of 2.8%. Offsite channels also performed well with a CTR of 1.8% and CVR of 1.2%. In contrast, CTV (Connected TV) showed minimal direct conversion (CVR 0.2%) and a lower CTR (0.7%), suggesting its primary role might be in brand awareness rather than direct response.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

Budget utilization varied by campaign objective. Conversion campaigns showed efficient pacing, utilizing 90% of their planned budget. Awareness campaigns slightly underspent at 93% utilization, while consideration campaigns were more conservative, using 88% of their budget. This suggests opportunities to re-evaluate budget allocation and potentially re-distribute funds to higher-performing objectives or channels, especially those driving direct conversions. The consistent growth in weekly revenue towards the end of the month indicates that retargeting efforts and promotional offers likely contributed to stronger mid-month and end-of-month conversion efficiency.

*   **Objective**: Identify actionable insights to replicate successful performance drivers and optimize future campaign timing and promotional strategies.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Action**: Re-evaluate budget allocation across campaign objectives, considering the efficient utilization of conversion budgets and the slightly conservative spend in awareness and consideration. Explore opportunities to shift budget towards high-performing conversion campaigns and channels (e.g., Onsite).
*   **Objective**: Maximize overall campaign ROI by ensuring resources are allocated to the most effective objectives and channels, potentially increasing spend in areas with proven high ROAS.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Action**: Given the strong performance of Onsite channels in terms of CTR and CVR, implement systematic A/B testing for creative variations, call-to-actions, and landing page experiences specifically for Onsite and high-performing Offsite placements.
*   **Objective**: Continuously improve user engagement and conversion rates by identifying the most effective creative and user experience elements, further enhancing the efficiency of direct response channels.
