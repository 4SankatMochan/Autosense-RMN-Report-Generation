## 5. Recommendations

### 5.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Action:** Conduct deeper analysis into daily fluctuations of CVR and ROAS, correlating them with specific campaign events, audience segments (e.g., Loyalty Program Members on Meta), and contextual placements. Investigate the days with higher CVR (e.g., 15% on 2025-08-09, 2025-08-10, 2025-08-13) to identify successful patterns and leverage these insights.
*   **Objective:** Optimize daily campaign settings and creative deployment to capitalize on peak performance periods and improve overall efficiency.

### 5.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Action:** Address the substantial overspend (1000% budget utilization) by implementing stricter budget controls and exploring dynamic bidding strategies that align with the campaign's planned budget while maintaining performance. A thorough review of the discrepancy between planned and actual spend is crucial to prevent future overruns.
*   **Objective:** Enhance budget efficiency and ensure campaigns operate within financial guidelines without compromising performance.

### 5.3. Refine Objective Alignment and Reporting

*   **Action:** Clarify the primary and secondary objectives of future campaigns at the planning stage. While this campaign was set for 'Consideration' and 'App Installs', the performance summary highlighted strong 'Sales' and 'Conversion' outcomes. Ensure reporting accurately reflects all intended objectives and their respective KPIs to provide a holistic view of campaign success.
*   **Objective:** Improve clarity in campaign planning and evaluation, allowing for more precise measurement of success against all desired outcomes and better strategic decision-making.
