## 3. Executive Summary

This report provides an analysis of Campaign ID 'CMP_2025_0003' for the brand 'Lux', which ran from August 1, 2025, to September 11, 2025, with a primary objective of 'Consideration'. The campaign utilized its full planned budget of $586.15, achieving 100% budget utilization.

For the month of August 2025, the campaign generated 65 conversions, demonstrating its ability to drive user actions. Performance metrics for the 'Onsite' channel, aligned with the 'Consideration' objective, show impressions ranging from 1,251 to 9,683, clicks between 22 and 52, and add-to-cart events from 64 to 845. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) varied, mostly around 0.9%, with some instances reaching 2.46% or 2.5%, and the Cost Per Click (CPC) ranged from $0.59 to $1.40.

Visualizations of monthly CTR and Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) trends are included to provide a dynamic view of performance over the campaign duration, which can inform future optimization strategies. No specific data was found for 'Awareness', 'Conversion' (as a primary objective), or 'Retention' objectives for this campaign.
