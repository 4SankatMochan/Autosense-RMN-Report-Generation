## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report details the performance of Dove's campaign **CMP_2025_0001**, which had a 'Consideration' objective with a sub-objective of 'App Installs'. The campaign, running from August 2 to September 6, 2025, had a planned spend of 494.49, which was fully utilized. The campaign primarily leveraged the 'Onsite' channel with 'Sponsored Product' media type.

### Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated consistent daily ad spend. Performance metrics showed variability, with impressions ranging from 1,339 to 9,431, unique reach from 334 to 3,876, and clicks from 19 to 55. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) varied between 0.9% and 1.72%, and Cost Per Click (CPC) ranged from 0.5 to 1.45.

### Channel Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was the sole channel reported, showing a total ad spend of 494.49, generating 96,550 impressions, 33,817 unique reach, and 546 clicks. The average CTR for this channel was 1.066%, with an average CPC of 0.974. No viewed units were recorded for this channel.

### Optimization Insight

The consistent daily spend suggests a fixed budget allocation strategy. The fluctuations in impressions and unique reach indicate varying audience availability or engagement over time. Further analysis of these daily trends could reveal optimal periods for ad delivery to maximize reach and engagement for future campaigns.
