## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

The `CMP_2025_0003` campaign for Brand `Lux`, primarily focused on `Consideration` with a sub-objective of `App Installs`, demonstrated a mixed performance. While successful in achieving its primary install objective, there are notable areas for improving financial efficiency.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

The campaign successfully generated **65 app installs**, directly aligning with its sub-objective. It achieved **632 clicks** from **113,438 impressions**, indicating a reasonable engagement level and a conversion rate of approximately 10.28% from clicks to installs. However, the campaign's financial efficiency shows room for improvement, with an **ROAS of 0.865** and an `Actual Spend` of **$5,861.50** against an `Attributed Sales Value` of **$5,072.69**, meaning it spent more than it directly generated in sales. The `Cost Per Install` (CPI) stands at approximately **$90.18**.

Monthly conversion rates for the campaign (observed in 2023) showed a consistent upward trend, increasing from 7.0% to 12.5%, mirrored by a rise in conversion counts from 140 to 250. The monthly Click-Through Rate (CTR) for 2024 remained generally stable around 5.00%, with a slight dip in March before recovering to a high of 5.09% in April.

### 3.3. Channel and Format Performance

The campaign utilized the `Onsite` channel with `Sponsored Product` media type, which contributed to the observed impressions, clicks, and add-to-cart metrics. The `Onsite` channel delivered 113,438 impressions, 37,086 unique reach, and 632 clicks, resulting in a CTR of 1.07% and a CPC of $0.99. It also recorded 6,901 add-to-cart events.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

There is a clear opportunity to optimize the campaign's financial efficiency and return on ad spend. Evaluating the `Cost Per Install` ($90.18) against the lifetime value (LTV) of an installed user is crucial for determining long-term viability and identifying strategies for more cost-effective installs. Despite achieving the app install objective, the current ROAS indicates that the campaign is not yet profitable from a direct sales perspective, suggesting a need to connect app installs more effectively to future revenue generation.
