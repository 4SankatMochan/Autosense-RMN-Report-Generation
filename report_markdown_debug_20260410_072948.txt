## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report details the performance of Campaign ID CMP_2025_0011 for Ben & Jerry's, focusing on the 'Consideration' objective. The campaign, named 'Launch', ran from June 12, 2025, to July 4, 2025, primarily utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media type on the 'Onsite' channel. The analysis provides insights into key performance indicators, daily trends, and observed anomalies to guide future marketing and sales strategies.

### Overall Performance

For Campaign ID `CMP_2025_0011` for Ben & Jerry's, the overall performance indicates a highly efficient and effective campaign, particularly in driving conversions and a strong return on investment.

**Key KPI Performance:**
*   **Spend Utilization:** The campaign fully utilized its planned budget of `1680.39`, achieving `100.00%` utilization (in terms of actual spend against the allocated budget for the period).
*   **Reach & Engagement:** It generated `137,188` impressions, reaching `48,429` unique users, and resulting in `1,891` clicks with a `CTR` of `1.38%`. The `CPC` was `0.89`.
*   **Conversion & Profitability:** The campaign yielded `200` conversions at an impressive `Conversion Rate` of `10.58%` and a favorable `CPA` of `8.40`. Most notably, the `Aggregated ROAS` was very strong at `8.94`, indicating a high return on ad spend.
*   **Purchase Intent:** There were `6,776` Add to Cart Events, significantly higher than the actual conversions, suggesting strong initial interest. `79` of the conversions were from new customers, indicating success in acquiring new brand loyalists.

### Channel Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was the primary focus for this campaign, demonstrating strong engagement metrics. For campaign ID 'CMP_2025_0011' and brand 'Ben & Jerry's', the 'Onsite' channel had a total daily spend of 1680.39, 137188 impressions, a reach of 48429, and 1891 clicks. The average click-through rate (CTR) was approximately 1.43%, and the average cost per click (CPC) was approximately 0.99.

### Optimization Insight

The significant discrepancy between "Add to Cart" events (`6,776`) and actual conversions (`200`) highlights a potential area for conversion funnel optimization. Additionally, the reported zero values for all video-related metrics suggest that either video assets were not part of this campaign or received no engagement, which should be investigated if video was intended to be a component.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

**Insights:** The significant difference between Add to Cart events (6,776) and actual conversions (200) suggests a potential drop-off point in the conversion funnel. Understanding the daily fluctuations in reach and impressions, alongside consistent daily spend, indicates that external factors or creative performance may be influencing engagement.
**Action:** Conduct a detailed analysis of the user journey from Add to Cart to conversion to identify friction points. Investigate the specific dates with peaks and troughs in reach and impressions to correlate with creative changes, competitor activity, or external events.
**Objective:** Improve conversion rates and optimize engagement.
**Timeline:** Immediate, within the next 2 weeks.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

**Insights:** The campaign achieved a strong ROAS of 8.94 and 100% utilization of its effective budget (1680.39), but the overall planned budget (38648.97) was significantly underutilized (4.35%). This indicates an opportunity to scale successful strategies.
**Action:** Re-evaluate the initial planned budget versus actual spend to understand future budget allocation potential. Implement dynamic bidding strategies to capitalize on peak performance days or hours identified through trend analysis, potentially increasing overall spend and maximizing ROAS.
**Objective:** Maximize ROI and campaign scale.
**Timeline:** Ongoing, with weekly adjustments.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

**Insights:** Given the high purchase intent (Add to Cart events) but lower conversion rate, there's a clear opportunity to optimize the final stages of the user journey. The "Not available" status for video metrics also suggests either a missed opportunity or a need to confirm asset usage.
**Action:** Conduct A/B tests on landing page elements, call-to-action buttons, and checkout processes to improve conversion rates. If video assets were intended, verify their implementation and performance tracking. If not, explore incorporating video to potentially boost engagement.
**Objective:** Enhance conversion funnel efficiency and explore new engagement formats.
**Timeline:** Continuous, starting with high-impact elements.
