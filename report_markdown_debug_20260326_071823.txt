## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report analyzes Campaign ID CMP_2025_0011 for the brand Onnit, which focused on a 'Consideration' objective. The campaign utilized an 'Onsite' channel with 'Sponsored Product' media type. The analysis covers campaign details, performance metrics, and weekly spending patterns to provide actionable insights for optimizing future marketing efforts.

### Overall Performance

Campaign CMP_2025_0011 achieved 100% budget utilization, with both planned and actual spend totaling 210.49. For the 'Onsite' channel, the campaign generated 84,053 impressions and 221 clicks. This resulted in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 0.26% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 8.57.

### Channel/Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was the sole channel reported for this campaign, demonstrating its role in delivering impressions and clicks towards the 'Consideration' objective. The 'Sponsored Product' media type effectively reached 27,440 unique users.

### Optimization Insight

Analysis of weekly ad spend revealed fluctuations throughout the campaign period, with spending ranging from 990 to 1300. This dynamic spending pattern suggests opportunities for optimizing budget allocation. Aligning spending more consistently with peak engagement periods or adjusting budgets based on real-time performance could enhance efficiency and impact.
