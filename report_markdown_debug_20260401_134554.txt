## 2. Executive Summary

### 2.1. Overview

This report details the performance of Campaign CMP_2025_0001 for the brand Dove, which ran from August 2, 2025, to September 6, 2025. The campaign's primary objective was 'Consideration', with a sub-objective of 'App Installs', utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media on 'Onsite' channels. This analysis aims to provide key insights for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to inform future strategic decisions.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated a strong Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 8.76, indicating a highly effective use of the advertising budget to generate sales. A healthy conversion rate of 10.26% suggests that users who clicked on the ads were very likely to complete a desired action, such as a purchase. The total spend for the campaign was $494.49, which resulted in $4,332.56 in attributed sales value from 56 conversions.

### 2.3. Channel and Format Performance

The campaign exclusively utilized the 'Onsite' channel with a 'Sponsored Product' media type. Daily ad spend on this channel remained consistent at approximately $27.47. Daily impressions varied significantly, reaching a high of 9,431 on August 18th. While the overall Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 0.57% was relatively low, the strong conversion rate and ROAS highlight the effectiveness of the 'Sponsored Product' format on the 'Onsite' channel in driving desired actions and sales.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

Despite the strong ROAS and conversion rate, the overall Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 0.57% was relatively low. This suggests an opportunity to optimize ad creatives or targeting strategies to enhance initial ad engagement and attract more clicks. Improving CTR could potentially lead to even greater overall campaign efficiency and higher total conversions, building upon the already strong post-click performance.
