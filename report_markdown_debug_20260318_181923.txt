## 2. Executive Summary

This report details the performance of Campaign ID 'CMP_2025_0003' for the brand 'Lux', primarily focused on a 'Consideration' objective with a sub-objective of 'App Installs'. The campaign, utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media on an 'Onsite' channel, ran from August 1, 2025, to September 11, 2025.

The campaign significantly overspent its planned budget of 586.15, with an actual spend of 5861.5, indicating a budget utilization of 1000%. For its 'Consideration' objective, the campaign generated 113,438 Impressions, reached 37,086 Unique Users, and achieved 632 Clicks, resulting in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 0.557% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.927. Notably, there were 6,901 Add To Cart events.

Monthly performance analysis reveals 63 conversions in August 2025. Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) in August 2025 varied across audience segments, with 'Frequent Browsers' showing the highest average ROAS at $10.48, followed by 'Loyalty Program Members' at $9.02. 'Brand Interactors' exhibited the lowest average ROAS at $7.81. Data for 'Awareness', 'Conversion', and 'Retention' objectives were not available, limiting a holistic view of the campaign's impact across the full marketing funnel.
