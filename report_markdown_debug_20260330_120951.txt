## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

The `CMP_2025_0001` campaign for the brand `Dove`, with an objective of 'Consideration' and sub-objective 'App Installs', has concluded with a 100% budget utilization, matching its planned spend of $494.49. The campaign, primarily leveraging 'Sponsored Product' media types on the 'Onsite' channel, ran for 35 days.

### Overall Performance

The campaign delivered a total of 19,030,000 impressions, reaching 20,210,000 unique users, resulting in 246,000 clicks and 24,200 conversions. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) stood at 1.29%, with a Conversion Rate (CVR) of 9.84%. The Cost Per Click (CPC) was $13.01, and the Cost Per Mille (CPM) was $168.16. These metrics indicate a robust performance in driving user consideration and subsequent app installs.

### Channel Format Performance

For the 'Onsite' channel, which was the primary channel for this campaign, the performance metrics were: Total Ad Spend of $494.49, 96,550 Impressions, 3,876 Unique Reach, and 546 Clicks. The CTR for this channel was 0.57%, and the CPC was $0.91. The Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) was not available.

### Optimization Insight

The campaign successfully utilized its entire budget, achieving its consideration objective. The high conversion rate and substantial reach suggest effective targeting and creative execution. Further optimization could focus on analyzing the weekly trends of spend and impressions to identify periods of higher engagement and allocate resources accordingly, as well as investigating the "Not available" CPCV to ensure all relevant metrics are tracked for future campaigns.
