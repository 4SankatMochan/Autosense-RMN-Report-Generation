## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report details the performance of Sunsilk's campaign `CMP_2025_0024`, focusing on key metrics such as ad spend, impressions, reach, and clicks, primarily on the 'Onsite' channel. The campaign's objective is inferred to be 'Consideration' given the available data.

### Overall Performance

The campaign on the 'Onsite' channel recorded a total daily spend of 371.79, generating 63,133 impressions, 21,907 reach, and 425 clicks. The average click-through rate (CTR) was approximately 1.13%, with an average cost per click (CPC) of about 0.95. Daily ad spend remained consistent at $26.56 from August 6 to August 19, 2025, indicating a stable budget allocation during this period.

### Channel Format Performance

Performance metrics show a range of values over the campaign duration, highlighting daily fluctuations. Impressions varied significantly, peaking at 9673 around August 16th, while clicks ranged from 18 to 51, and CTR from 0.9% to 2.5%. This variability suggests dynamic engagement patterns.

### Optimization Insight

The consistent daily ad spend, coupled with fluctuating impressions and reach, indicates that while budget allocation was stable, external factors or campaign settings might have influenced daily delivery. The significant variations in reach and impressions suggest opportunities for optimizing ad delivery schedules or targeting to maximize exposure during periods of higher potential engagement.
