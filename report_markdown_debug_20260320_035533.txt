## 3. Executive Summary

This report provides an in-depth analysis of the 'Summer Refresh' campaign (CMP_2025_0001) for the Dove brand, focusing on its 'Consideration' objective. The campaign, which ran from August 2, 2025, to September 6, 2025, aimed to drive app installs through various channels.

### 3.1. Overview

The campaign utilized a planned budget of $500,000, with a total ad spend of $325,000, achieving a budget utilization of 65%. The analysis of daily and weekly performance metrics reveals a stable and consistent performance, with no significant anomalies detected.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

Across the analyzed period, the campaign demonstrated consistent engagement. On average, it generated approximately 11,482 impressions and reached about 9,171 unique users daily. The average daily clicks stood at 232, resulting in an average Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 2.34%. Cost metrics indicate an average Cost Per Click (CPC) of $0.39 and an average Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) of $0.40, although the median CPCV is significantly lower at $0.06, suggesting variability in video completion costs.

### 3.3. Channel Format Performance

A detailed breakdown by channel for the 'Consideration' objective highlights varying efficiencies:
*   **Social** channels demonstrated the highest engagement with the highest impressions, clicks, and CTR (0.015), alongside the lowest CPC ($1.33).
*   **Display** channels showed strong reach but with a higher CPC ($1.75) and CPCV ($175) compared to Social.
*   **Video** channels, while contributing to reach, exhibited the highest CPC ($5.00) and a significantly high CPCV ($2500), indicating potential areas for optimization in video ad placements or creative.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

The consistent performance across weeks and the absence of significant anomalies suggest a well-managed campaign. However, the disparity in CPCV across channels, particularly the high cost for video views, points to an opportunity for optimizing video content and targeting. Leveraging the strong performance of social channels could further enhance overall campaign efficiency.

## 6. Recommendations

Based on the analysis of Campaign CMP_2025_0001, the following recommendations are provided to optimize future marketing efforts:

### 6.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

**Action:** Conduct a deeper dive into the daily and weekly performance trends, specifically analyzing ad spend and engagement metrics against content themes, audience segments, and time-of-day performance.
**Objective:** To identify optimal timings and contextual placements for ad delivery that maximize engagement and minimize cost, especially for channels with higher CPC/CPCV like Video.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

**Action:** Reallocate budget based on channel performance, favoring high-efficiency channels like Social. Review and adjust bidding strategies for Video campaigns to reduce Cost Per Completed View (CPCV).
**Objective:** To improve overall campaign ROI by directing spend towards channels that deliver better engagement metrics for the 'Consideration' objective, and to bring down the cost of video views.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

**Action:** Implement A/B testing for different ad creatives, ad formats, and landing page experiences, particularly for Video and Display channels. Focus on elements that influence CTR and user progression towards app installs.
**Objective:** To enhance the effectiveness of ad content and improve the user journey, thereby increasing Click-Through Rates (CTR) and reducing Cost Per Click (CPC) and Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) across all channels.
