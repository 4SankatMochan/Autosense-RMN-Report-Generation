## 2. Executive Summary

This report details the performance of Campaign ID 'CMP_2025_0003' for the 'Lux' brand, which focused on a 'Consideration' objective with 'App Installs' as a sub-objective, utilizing 'Onsite' channels and 'Sponsored Product' media type.

### 2.1. Overview

The campaign ran from August 1, 2025, to September 11, 2025, with a planned budget of 9088.36 and an actual spend of 5861.5. It generated significant engagement, including 113,438 impressions, 37,086 unique reach, and 632 clicks, resulting in an average CTR of 1.066%. A notable outcome was 6,901 add-to-cart events.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

The campaign successfully drove consideration, as evidenced by the substantial impressions, reach, and add-to-cart events. The budget utilization for the specific summary provided was 100% of a 586.15 budget. Monthly conversion analysis shows varying contributions from different audience segments.

### 2.3. Channel and Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel, specifically using 'Sponsored Product' media, was the sole channel for this campaign. Performance varied significantly across audience segments within this channel. 'Frequent Browsers' consistently demonstrated the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) at 2.5% in August 2025 and also showed the highest monthly Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) trend from August to October 2025. 'Brand Interactors' on 'Onsite' had a CTR of 1.29%, while 'App Engaged Shoppers', 'Category Enthusiasts', and 'Loyalty Program Members' all showed a lower CTR of 0.9%.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

The analysis indicates that 'Frequent Browsers' are a highly engaged and profitable segment, yielding the best CTR and ROAS. Conversely, 'Brand Interactors' generally showed the lowest ROAS. Optimizing targeting and creative for these distinct segments within the 'Onsite' channel could further enhance campaign efficiency. Due to data availability for August 2025 only for CTR, a long-term trend analysis for CTR was not possible, which limits deeper insights into its evolution.
