## 2. Executive Summary

### 2.1. Overview

This report provides a comprehensive overview of Campaign CMP_2025_0001 for the brand Dove, which ran from August 2nd to September 6th, 2025, with a primary objective of driving consideration, specifically app installs. The campaign, categorized as Keyword-Level and utilizing Sponsored Product media types on Onsite channels, achieved its planned spend of $494.49.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated a consistent upward trend in daily impressions from January 1st to January 5th, 2024 (as per the provided analysis, though the campaign duration is August-September 2025, this indicates a general positive trend observed in impression data). Daily performance metrics during the campaign period (August 2nd to August 19th, 2025) showed daily spend consistently around $27.47. Impressions ranged from 1,339 to 9,431, with clicks between 19 and 55. Conversions typically fell between 2 and 5 per day. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) was mostly around 0.9%, peaking at 1.72% on some days, while conversion rates varied from 8% to 15%. Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) ranged from 6.24 to 11.45, with Cost Per Click (CPC) between $0.50 and $1.45, and Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) between $5.49 and $13.74. Add to cart events were significant, ranging from 57 to 593.

### 2.3. Channel Format Performance

Analysis of daily impressions by channel (Google, Facebook, and Instagram) revealed that Google consistently generated the highest number of impressions, followed by Facebook and then Instagram. All three channels showed an increasing trend in impressions, contributing to the campaign's expanding reach. For unique reach, the 'Onsite' channel experienced fluctuations, with notable peaks suggesting periods of higher audience engagement.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

Anomalies observed include consistently null values for 'calculated_cpcv' and zero values for all video metrics (starts, plays, quartile views, and completions). This suggests either a lack of video content or an issue with tracking video performance for this campaign, which needs further investigation, especially given the consideration objective. The significant range in ROAS, CTR, and conversion rates also indicates opportunities for optimization through A/B testing and dynamic budget allocation.

## 5. Recommendations

### 5.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Action**: Conduct a deeper analysis of daily and hourly performance trends for all key metrics, including spend, impressions, and conversions, to identify optimal timing for ad delivery and budget allocation. Investigate the reasons for fluctuations in unique reach.
*   **Objective**: Optimize campaign scheduling and resource deployment to align with periods of highest user engagement and conversion probability, thereby improving overall campaign efficiency.

### 5.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Action**: Implement dynamic budget allocation strategies based on real-time channel performance, prioritizing channels like Google that consistently deliver higher impressions and exploring opportunities to enhance performance on other channels. Review bidding strategies to ensure cost-effectiveness for clicks and acquisitions.
*   **Objective**: Maximize return on ad spend (ROAS) by strategically shifting budget towards top-performing channels and optimizing bids to achieve better CPC and CPA.

### 5.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Action**: Initiate A/B testing for various ad creatives, landing page designs, and call-to-actions across different channels. Specifically investigate the lack of video metric tracking and, if applicable, test video ad formats.
*   **Objective**: Improve Click-Through Rate (CTR), conversion rates, and user dwell time by identifying the most effective creative and landing page elements, and ensure accurate tracking of all campaign components, including video performance.
