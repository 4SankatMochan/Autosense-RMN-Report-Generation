# Campaign Performance Report

## 1. Context

This report provides an overview of campaign performance for Dove brand initiatives during July 2025. The analysis focuses on key metrics such as Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Click-Through Rate (CTR), Conversion Rate (CVR), and budget utilization across various campaigns and channels. The insights aim to inform strategic decisions for optimizing future marketing spend and resource allocation.

The top 5 campaigns by ROAS for July 2025 were:
*   **CMP_2025_0001 (Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch)**: ROAS 6.6
*   **CMP_2025_0002 (Dove Deodorant Awareness)**: ROAS 1.2
*   **CMP_2025_0003 (Dove Hair Therapy)**: ROAS 4.8
*   **CMP_2025_0004 (Dove Men+Care Range)**: ROAS 3.9
*   **CMP_2025_0005 (Dove Intense Repair Shampoo)**: ROAS 3.6

These campaigns collectively contributed approximately 80% of the total attributed sales for the brand during the reporting period.

## 3. Executive Summary

**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive overview of Dove's campaign performance in July 2025, focusing on ROAS, channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and revenue generation. The primary objective was to understand channel contributions to attributed sales and daily sales patterns to optimize future marketing spend and maximize ROI.

*   **Overall Performance**: Dove campaigns demonstrated strong performance in July 2025, with daily ROAS consistently above 5.5 and peaking at 7.0 around July 15th. Weekly conversions and revenue showed consistent growth, with revenue reaching $17.6K in Week 4. The top 5 campaigns contributed significantly, accounting for 80% of total attributed sales.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising proved to be the most effective channel, achieving the highest CTR (3.0%) and CVR (2.8%). Offsite channels showed moderate performance, while CTV primarily contributed to awareness with minimal direct conversion.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Budget utilization was efficient across campaign objectives. Conversion campaigns utilized 90% of their budget, Awareness campaigns 93%, and Consideration campaigns 88%, indicating well-managed spending.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: Consistent week-over-week growth in conversions and revenue suggests improving campaign efficiency and stronger shopper intent over time, particularly towards the end of the month.
*   **Top Performing Segments**: The "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign (ROAS 6.6) was the top-performing campaign. Onsite channels demonstrated superior engagement and conversion efficiency.
*   **Underperforming Areas**: CTV showed minimal direct conversion (CVR 0.2%), suggesting its primary role is brand awareness rather than direct sales. The "Dove Deodorant Awareness" campaign had a comparatively lower ROAS of 1.2.

## Campaigns Comparison

**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: Campaigns with clear conversion objectives, such as "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch," achieved significantly higher ROAS (6.6) compared to awareness-focused campaigns like "Dove Deodorant Awareness" (ROAS 1.2). This highlights the importance of aligning campaign design with specific performance expectations.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: Budget utilization by objective was generally efficient, with conversion campaigns showing strong pacing (90% utilized). This indicates effective financial management and resource allocation towards high-impact goals.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for direct conversions and engagement, while CTV serves a critical role in awareness, despite lower direct conversion metrics. A balanced strategy leveraging both direct-response and awareness channels is crucial.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: While some campaigns excel in ROAS, others may contribute to broader brand objectives like awareness. For instance, the "Dove Deodorant Awareness" campaign, despite lower ROAS, likely contributed to top-of-funnel metrics not directly captured by ROAS.
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to prioritize and optimize high-ROAS campaigns and onsite channels for direct sales. Simultaneously, evaluate the indirect impact and brand lift generated by awareness-focused channels like CTV to ensure a holistic understanding of their value.
