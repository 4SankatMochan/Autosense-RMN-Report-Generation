## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report provides a comprehensive overview of the performance of Campaign ID `CMP_2025_0003` for the brand `Lux`, which had a primary objective of 'Consideration'. The campaign utilized 'Sponsored Product' media type on the 'Onsite' channel, running from August 1, 2025, to September 11, 2025.

### Overall Performance

The campaign had a planned budget of 586.15, with an actual spend of 5861.5. The budget utilization was 100% based on the provided summary, though the actual spend significantly exceeded the planned budget. For August 2025, the monthly average Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) was 8.65, indicating a strong return on investment.

### Channel/Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel was a key performer, generating 113,438 Impressions and reaching 37,086 Unique users. It achieved 632 Clicks, resulting in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.066% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.989. Critically, the campaign drove 6,901 'Add To Cart' events, aligning with its 'Consideration' objective.

### Optimization Insight

Analysis of monthly CTR variations reveals that 'Onsite - Frequent Browsers' demonstrated the highest CTR at 2.5% in August 2025, significantly outperforming other segments like 'Onsite - App Engaged Shoppers', 'Onsite - Category Enthusiasts', and 'Onsite - Loyalty Program Members' (all at 0.9%). 'Onsite - Brand Interactors' also performed well with a CTR of 1.29%. This highlights the importance of audience segmentation for optimizing engagement.
