## 2. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive overview of Dove campaign performance during July 2025, prepared for the Marketing and Sales leadership team. The analysis focuses on understanding channel effectiveness, campaign efficiency, budget utilization, and overall sales patterns to inform strategic decisions for optimizing future marketing spend and maximizing ROI.

### 2.1. Overall Performance

The top 5 campaigns by Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) for July 2025 were led by "CMP_2025_0001 (Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch)" with an impressive ROAS of 6.6, followed by "CMP_2025_0003 (Dove Hair Therapy)" at 4.8. These top campaigns collectively contributed approximately 80% of total attributed sales. The daily ROAS trend for Dove campaigns remained strong throughout July, consistently above 5.5 and peaking at 7.0 around July 15, indicating robust mid-month conversion efficiency. Weekly performance showed consistent growth, with conversions peaking in Week 4 and attributed revenue reaching $17.6K, suggesting improving campaign efficiency and stronger shopper intent over time.

### 2.2. Channel Format Performance

Onsite advertising demonstrated the highest engagement and conversion efficiency, achieving a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 3.0% and a Conversion Rate (CVR) of 2.8%. Offsite channels also performed well with a CTR of 1.8% and CVR of 1.2%. In contrast, Connected TV (CTV) exhibited minimal direct conversion (CTR 0.7%, CVR 0.2%), primarily contributing to brand awareness rather than immediate sales.

### 2.3. Optimization Insight

Budget utilization analysis revealed that Conversion campaigns were highly efficient, utilizing 90% of their allocated budget. Awareness campaigns slightly underspent at 93% utilization, while Consideration campaigns were more conservative, utilizing 88% of their budget. This suggests effective pacing for conversion-focused efforts and potential room for increased investment in awareness and consideration phases to drive further growth.

## 5. Recommendations

Based on the analysis of Dove campaign performance in July 2025, the following recommendations are put forth to further optimize marketing efforts and maximize ROI.

### 5.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Action:** Investigate the specific factors contributing to the ROAS peak around July 15 (e.g., specific promotions, media placements, or audience segments active during that period). Analyze the content and targeting strategies of "CMP_2025_0001 (Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch)" to understand its high ROAS (6.6) and identify transferable best practices.
*   **Objective:** To replicate and scale successful campaign elements and capitalize on optimal timing for future campaigns, thereby enhancing overall campaign efficiency.

### 5.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Action:** Given the high efficiency of Conversion campaigns (90% budget utilization) and the consistent week-over-week growth in conversions and revenue, consider reallocating a portion of the slightly underspent Awareness (93% utilization) and Consideration (88% utilization) budgets towards Conversion objectives, especially during periods of high ROAS.
*   **Objective:** To maximize immediate ROI by funneling resources into proven high-performing conversion pathways, while maintaining a strategic spend on awareness and consideration to build future pipeline.

### 5.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Action:** Conduct A/B tests on onsite ad creatives and landing page experiences, given their superior CTR (3.0%) and CVR (2.8%). For CTV campaigns, explore different creative formats and calls-to-action that might encourage direct engagement or drive traffic to other conversion-focused channels, even if direct conversion remains low.
*   **Objective:** To continuously improve the performance of high-converting channels and explore new ways to leverage awareness-focused channels for incremental gains in engagement and conversion.
