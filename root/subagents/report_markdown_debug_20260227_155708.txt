## 3. Executive Summary
**Overview**: This report analyzes the performance of Dove campaigns during July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and weekly conversion trends. The top 5 campaigns contributed significantly to attributed sales, with "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" showing exceptional ROAS.
*   **Overall Performance**: Dove campaigns demonstrated strong ROAS, consistently above 5.5 throughout July, peaking at 7.0 mid-month. The top 5 campaigns by ROAS contributed approximately 80% of total attributed sales for the brand. Weekly conversions and revenue showed consistent growth, with revenue peaking at $17.6K in Week 4.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite ads exhibited the highest engagement and conversion efficiency with a CTR of 3.0% and CVR of 2.8%. Offsite channels also performed reasonably well, while CTV primarily contributed to awareness with lower direct conversion metrics.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Budget utilization was efficient across objectives, with Conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budget. The mid-month ROAS peak suggests effective retargeting and promotional offers.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: Onsite channels show strong mid-funnel engagement with high CTR and CVR. While specific mid-funnel metrics are not explicitly detailed, the superior performance of onsite channels indicates that users engaging directly with the platform are highly motivated to convert.

### Campaigns Comparison
**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: The data suggests a clear distinction in performance based on implied campaign objectives. The "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" (ROAS 6.6) likely had a strong conversion focus, while "Dove Deodorant Awareness" (ROAS 1.2) aligns with an awareness objective. The overall budget utilization by objective shows Conversion campaigns using 90% of their budget, indicating a focused approach.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: Overall, budget utilization by objective was efficient, with Awareness, Conversion, and Consideration campaigns utilizing 93%, 90%, and 88% of their planned budgets respectively. This indicates good pacing and management of ad spend.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for direct engagement and conversion (CTR 3.0%, CVR 2.8%), suggesting that investing in the user experience and calls-to-action on owned properties yields strong results. Offsite channels also contribute to conversions (CTR 1.8%, CVR 1.2%), while CTV primarily serves an awareness function (CTR 0.7%, CVR 0.2%).
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There's a trade-off between awareness-driven campaigns (lower ROAS but broader reach) and conversion-driven campaigns (higher ROAS but potentially narrower targeting). The peak in daily ROAS mid-month suggests that strategic timing of promotions or retargeting efforts can significantly boost performance.
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to differentiate strategies based on campaign objectives, leveraging high-ROAS campaigns for direct sales and awareness campaigns for brand building, while closely monitoring their respective performance metrics.

## 6. Recommendations

### 1. Conduct Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis
**Action**: Investigate the factors contributing to the daily ROAS peak around July 15th. This could involve analyzing specific creative, targeting, or promotional activities active during that period.
**Objective**: Identify actionable insights from peak performance periods to replicate successful strategies and optimize campaign timing for future initiatives.

### 2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization
**Action**: Based on the observed ROAS for top campaigns and the budget utilization by objective, implement a dynamic budget allocation model that shifts resources towards high-performing campaigns and channels, especially those driving conversions.
**Objective**: Maximize overall ROI by continuously reallocating budget to the most efficient campaign objectives and channels, while ensuring adequate funding for awareness-building efforts.

### 3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements
**Action**: Conduct A/B tests for creative variations and landing page experiences, particularly for onsite and offsite channels, to further improve CTR and CVR.
**Objective**: Enhance user engagement and conversion rates by identifying the most effective creative and user experience elements across different media channels.
