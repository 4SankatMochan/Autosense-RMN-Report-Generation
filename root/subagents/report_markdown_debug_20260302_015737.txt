## 3. Executive Summary
**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive overview of Dove's campaign performance during July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and conversion trends. The analysis highlights key areas for optimizing future marketing spend and campaign targeting.

*   **Overall Performance**: Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch campaign (CMP_2025_0001) demonstrated exceptional ROAS at 6.6, significantly outperforming other campaigns. Overall daily ROAS remained strong, consistently above 5.5 throughout July, peaking at 7.0 mid-month. Total attributed conversions and revenue showed a positive trend, with revenue peaking in Week 4 at $17.6K.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising proved to be the most effective channel, achieving the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 3.0% and Conversion Rate (CVR) of 2.8%. Offsite channels showed moderate performance, while CTV primarily contributed to awareness with minimal direct conversion.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Campaign budget utilization was efficient across objectives, with Conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their budget. The high ROAS of specific campaigns and the strong performance of onsite channels suggest opportunities for reallocating resources to maximize ROI.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: The consistent week-over-week growth in conversions and revenue, particularly in the latter half of July, indicates improving campaign efficiency and stronger shopper intent over time, suggesting effective mid-funnel strategies.

Understanding which campaigns deliver the highest Return on Ad Spend is crucial for strategic allocation. The top 5 campaigns by ROAS for July 2025 were:

## Campaigns Comparison
**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: Campaigns like "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" (CMP_2025_0001) with a high ROAS (6.6) demonstrate excellent alignment between campaign execution and desired outcomes, likely driving significant attributed sales. The overall budget utilization by objective (90% for Conversion, 93% for Awareness, 88% for Consideration) indicates a generally well-paced spending strategy across different strategic goals.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: The efficient utilization of budgets, particularly for conversion-focused campaigns, suggests that resources are being deployed effectively. However, the slight underspending in Awareness and Consideration objectives could present an opportunity for re-evaluation or reallocation if additional investment is deemed beneficial for long-term brand building or lead nurturing.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for direct conversions and engagement, as evidenced by their superior CTR and CVR. Offsite channels play a role in traffic generation, while CTV primarily serves awareness. This suggests a multi-channel strategy where each channel contributes differently to the marketing funnel.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There's a clear trade-off between awareness-focused channels (like CTV) and conversion-focused channels (like Onsite). While CTV may not drive direct conversions, its contribution to brand visibility could indirectly support the higher conversion rates seen in other channels. The strong daily ROAS trend and weekly revenue growth indicate that the overall campaign mix is driving positive financial outcomes.
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to invest in and optimize high-ROAS campaigns and onsite channels. Explore opportunities to enhance the direct response capabilities of offsite channels and leverage CTV for its awareness-building strengths, potentially with more integrated measurement of its indirect impact.
