# Campaign Performance Report

## 1. Context

This report is prepared for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to provide a comprehensive overview of campaign performance across different channels. The primary objective is to understand which channels contribute most effectively to attributed sales and to identify overall daily sales patterns. The insights derived from this analysis will be crucial in informing strategic decisions related to optimizing future marketing spend, resource allocation, and campaign targeting to maximize ROI.

## 2. Customization Options
This report can be filtered and customized along the following dimensions:
* Timeline: Daily, Weekly
* By Creative: Channel, Platform, Format

## 3. Executive Summary

**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive analysis of Dove campaign performance for July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and weekly conversion and revenue trends. The primary objective is to inform strategic decisions for optimizing future marketing spend and campaign targeting.

*   **Overall Performance**: Dove campaigns demonstrated strong ROAS, with the "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign achieving an impressive 6.6 ROAS. Overall daily ROAS for Dove campaigns remained consistently above 5.5 throughout July, peaking at 7.0 mid-month. These campaigns collectively contributed approximately 80% of total attributed sales for the brand.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite channels exhibited the highest engagement and conversion efficiency (CTR 3.0%, CVR 2.8%). Offsite channels also contributed, while CTV primarily served for awareness with minimal direct conversion.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Budget utilization was efficient across objectives, with conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budget. Weekly conversions and revenue showed a positive trend, with both peaking in Week 4, indicating improving campaign efficiency over time.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: While specific mid-funnel engagement metrics are not detailed, the strong ROAS and increasing weekly conversions suggest effective user progression through the sales funnel, particularly for returning users or those exposed to retargeting efforts.

## 5. Detailed Performance Analysis

### 5.1. Top Performing Campaigns by ROAS

During July 2025, the following campaigns demonstrated the highest Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), significantly contributing to the brand's attributed sales:

*   **CMP_2025_0001 (Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch):** ROAS 6.6
*   **CMP_2025_0003 (Dove Hair Therapy):** ROAS 4.8
*   **CMP_2025_0004 (Dove Men+Care Range):** ROAS 3.9
*   **CMP_2025_0005 (Dove Intense Repair Shampoo):** ROAS 3.6
*   **CMP_2025_0002 (Dove Deodorant Awareness):** ROAS 1.2

These top 5 campaigns were highly effective, collectively contributing approximately 80% of total attributed sales for the brand. The "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign stands out with an exceptional ROAS of 6.6.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Conduct Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis
**Action**: Investigate the specific factors contributing to the ROAS peak around July 15 and the strong performance in Week 4. Analyze creative performance, audience segments, and promotional activities during these periods.
**Objective**: Identify actionable insights and best practices that can be replicated in future campaigns to consistently achieve higher ROAS and conversion rates.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization
**Action**: Based on the strong performance of conversion campaigns and the daily ROAS trends, consider dynamically reallocating budget towards high-performing campaigns and channels, especially during periods of peak efficiency. Optimize bidding strategies to capitalize on the higher engagement observed in Onsite channels.
**Objective**: Maximize overall campaign ROI by strategically shifting resources to areas demonstrating the highest return, ensuring budget is utilized where it generates the most value.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements
**Action**: Implement systematic A/B testing for creatives and landing pages, particularly for the top-performing campaigns like "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch," and for Onsite channels. Focus on elements that can further enhance CTR and CVR.
**Objective**: Continuously improve campaign effectiveness by identifying the most impactful creative and landing page elements, thereby driving higher engagement and conversion rates across the board.
