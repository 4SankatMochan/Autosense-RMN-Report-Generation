## 3. Executive Summary
**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive overview of Dove's campaign performance during July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and weekly revenue and conversion trends. *   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: Onsite channels exhibited strong mid-funnel engagement with high CTR and CVR, indicating that users interacting with onsite ads are highly engaged and conversion-ready. Offsite channels also contributed to engagement, albeit at a lower rate.

## Campaigns Comparison
**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: The budget utilization by objective (Awareness, Conversion, Consideration) indicates a strategic alignment of spend with campaign goals. Conversion campaigns, which are typically high-ROI, saw substantial budget allocation and efficient utilization.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: All campaign objectives demonstrated good budget efficiency, with utilization rates between 88% and 93%. This suggests effective financial planning and execution, avoiding significant over or underspending.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for driving direct conversions, while Offsite provides a broader reach with moderate engagement. CTV's lower direct conversion rates suggest its primary value lies in upper-funnel awareness and brand building rather than immediate sales.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There is a clear trade-off between reach-focused channels (like CTV) and conversion-focused channels (like Onsite). While CTV contributes to awareness, Onsite ads are crucial for converting engaged users. The high ROAS of specific product launch campaigns highlights the importance of targeted product-centric marketing.
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to leverage Onsite channels for high-intent users and direct conversions, while strategically using Offsite and CTV for broader reach and brand awareness. Optimize budget allocation to capitalize on high-performing campaigns and objectives.
