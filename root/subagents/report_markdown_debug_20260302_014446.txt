## 3. Executive Summary
**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive overview of Dove campaign performance for July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and conversion trends. The analysis highlights key areas of success and opportunities for optimization to maximize future marketing ROI.
* Overall Performance: Dove campaigns demonstrated strong ROAS throughout July 2025, with a peak of 7.0 around mid-month. The top 5 campaigns by ROAS contributed approximately 80% of total attributed sales, indicating efficient spend. Weekly conversions and revenue showed consistent growth, with revenue reaching $17.6K in Week 4.
* Channel/Format Performance: Onsite advertising proved to be the most effective channel, achieving the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 3.0% and Conversion Rate (CVR) of 2.8%. Offsite channels also performed well, while CTV primarily contributed to awareness with minimal direct conversion.
* Optimization Insight: Budget utilization was efficient across objectives, with Conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budget. The consistent ROAS above 5.5 suggests robust campaign management and effective targeting.
* Mid-Funnel Engagement: Onsite channels show strong mid-funnel engagement with high CTR and CVR, indicating that users interacting directly with the brand's platform are highly motivated.

### Campaign Analysis
The overall performance of Dove campaigns in July 2025 was robust. The top 5 campaigns by ROAS, including 'Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch' (ROAS 6.6) and 'Dove Hair Therapy' (ROAS 4.8), were significant contributors, accounting for approximately 80% of total attributed sales. This highlights the success of specific product-focused campaigns. The daily ROAS trend indicated consistent efficiency, with a strong peak mid-month. Channel performance varied, with Onsite advertising driving the highest engagement and conversions, suggesting a strong intent from users interacting directly with the brand's platform. While CTV's direct conversion metrics were lower, its role in awareness should be considered. Budget utilization was efficient across all objectives, with Conversion campaigns effectively using 90% of their allocated budget. The consistent growth in weekly conversions and revenue underscores the overall positive trajectory and effectiveness of the marketing efforts.

## Campaigns Comparison
**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: Campaigns effectively delivered on their objectives, particularly those focused on conversion, as evidenced by high ROAS and significant attributed sales from top-performing campaigns.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: Budget utilization was strong across all campaign objectives (Awareness: 93%, Conversion: 90%, Consideration: 88%), indicating effective budget planning and pacing throughout the month.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels demonstrated superior performance in terms of direct engagement (CTR) and conversion (CVR), suggesting that users on owned platforms are highly engaged. Offsite channels also contributed significantly, while CTV served more for awareness building.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There's a clear trade-off between awareness-focused channels like CTV (lower direct conversion but higher reach potential) and conversion-focused channels like Onsite (higher direct conversion).
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to leverage Onsite channels for high-intent conversions while strategically using Offsite and CTV for broader reach and awareness to nurture the top and middle of the funnel.

## 6. Recommendations

## 1. Conduct Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis
**Action**: Investigate the factors contributing to the ROAS peak around July 15th. Analyze specific creative, audience, and platform data from that period to identify repeatable success patterns.
**Objective**: To understand the drivers behind peak performance and replicate successful strategies for future campaigns, optimizing for timing and contextual relevance.

## 2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization
**Action**: Implement a dynamic budget allocation strategy that can shift resources more aggressively towards top-performing campaigns (e.g., 'Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch') and channels (e.g., Onsite) during periods of high efficiency, as observed in the daily ROAS trend.
**Objective**: To maximize Return on Ad Spend by reallocating budget to capitalize on real-time performance insights and ensure optimal resource utilization.

## 3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements
**Action**: Conduct A/B tests on creative variations and landing page experiences specifically for Onsite and Offsite channels to further improve CTR and CVR. For CTV, test different messaging and calls-to-action to better bridge awareness to consideration.
**Objective**: To continuously enhance user engagement and conversion rates across all channels by identifying the most effective creative and user journey elements.
