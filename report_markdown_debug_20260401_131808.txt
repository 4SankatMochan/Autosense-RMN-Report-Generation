The "CMP_2025_2158" campaign for Lifebuoy demonstrated strong financial efficiency and effective conversion generation, despite a relatively modest total spend. The campaign achieved an outstanding **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 7.36**, generating $5,980.86 in sales value from a total spend of $813.06. The **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) was highly efficient at $7.13**, indicating a low cost to acquire each conversion. It delivered significant visibility with **634,614 Total Impressions** and **134,820 Total Reach**. A strong **Conversion Rate of 12.46%** led to **114 Total Conversions**, with **33 new to brand conversions**, showcasing effective customer acquisition.

### Channel/Format Performance

The campaign was executed through **In-App Ads** on the **App** channel. While the **Click-Through Rate (CTR) was low at 0.14%**, resulting in 915 clicks, the **Cost Per Click (CPC) remained efficient at $0.89**. A remarkable **34,794 Total Add to Cart Events** were attributed to the campaign, alongside an **Average Dwell Time of 32.39 seconds**. This suggests that while direct clicks were moderate, the campaign had a substantial influence on user intent and deeper engagement on the platform, likely through view-through conversions or other attribution models. Video engagement metrics (starts, plays, completions, average watch time) were explicitly noted as not significant for this campaign.

### Optimization Insight

The campaign exhibits a trend of high return on investment and efficient conversion acquisition. The exceptionally high number of "Add to Cart Events" (34,794) compared to "Total Clicks" (915) is notable, indicating a significant influence beyond direct clicks. The primary area for review would be the lack of performance in video engagement, if that was a strategic objective. Otherwise, the campaign demonstrated robust performance in its core sales and conversion objectives.
