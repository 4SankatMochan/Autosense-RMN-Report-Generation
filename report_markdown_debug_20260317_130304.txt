## 2. Executive Summary

This report analyzes the performance of Campaign ID CMP_2025_0003 for the brand Lux, which ran from August 1, 2025, to September 11, 2025, with a primary objective of 'Consideration'. The campaign, executed through the 'Onsite' channel as a 'Sponsored Product', achieved its planned budget utilization.

### 2.1. Overview

The campaign aimed to drive user consideration, focusing on App Installs. Key metrics like monthly ROAS, conversions, and CTR were analyzed to assess effectiveness across various audience segments and platforms.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

The campaign successfully utilized its entire planned budget of 586.15. For the 'Consideration' objective, the 'Onsite' channel generated 113,438 Impressions, reached 37,086 Unique Users, and resulted in 632 Clicks, with a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 1.07%. Notably, it also drove 6,901 Add To Cart events. However, the Monthly Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) for the 'Onsite' channel in August 2025 was 0.865, indicating that the ad spend did not fully return its investment in sales for that specific month. Detailed analysis reveals varied performance across audience segments, with 'Brand Interactors' and 'Loyalty Program Members' showing strong engagement and conversions, while 'Frequent Browsers' demonstrated high ROAS (10.48) and low Cost Per Click (CPC) (0.69).

### 2.3. Channel and Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel, utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media type, was the sole channel for this campaign. While it generated significant impressions and clicks, the ROAS of 0.865 suggests room for improvement in sales attribution relative to spend. Conversion and CTR trends are available for monthly analysis, but specific channel comparisons are limited to the 'Onsite' channel due to the campaign's structure.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

Optimizations should focus on leveraging high-performing audience segments such as 'Frequent Browsers' for their efficient ad spend (high ROAS, low CPC). Continued efforts to drive volume and sales from 'Brand Interactors' and 'Loyalty Program Members' are also crucial, given their strong performance in clicks, conversions, and attributed sales value.
