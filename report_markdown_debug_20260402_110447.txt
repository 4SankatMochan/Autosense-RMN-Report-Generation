## 2. Executive Summary

This report details the performance of Campaign ID `CMP_2025_0005` for the brand `Kissan`, which aimed for a 'Consideration' objective via 'Onsite' channels using 'Sponsored Product' media. The campaign ran from July 29, 2025, to September 24, 2025.

The campaign significantly overspent its planned budget, with a spend utilization of 2.00 (200%), indicating actual spend was double the planned amount. Despite this, the campaign generated varying daily impressions, unique reach, and clicks within the 'Onsite' channel.

All reported performance metrics are attributed to the 'Onsite' channel and 'Sponsored Product' media type. The daily trends for total ad spend, unique reach, and impressions demonstrate dynamic activity within this single channel, with noticeable peaks and troughs.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

The significant overspend highlights a need for tighter budget management and potentially more dynamic allocation strategies. The daily fluctuations in key performance indicators (KPIs) suggest opportunities for optimizing ad delivery timing and creative based on observed engagement patterns.
