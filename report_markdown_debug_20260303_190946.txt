## 3. Executive Summary
**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive overview of campaign performance for Dove during July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and weekly conversion and revenue trends. The analysis aims to inform strategic decisions for optimizing future marketing spend and maximizing ROI.

*   **Overall Performance**: Dove's campaigns demonstrated strong performance in July 2025, with ROAS consistently above 5.5 and a peak of 7.0 mid-month. The top 5 campaigns contributed approximately 80% of total attributed sales, highlighting the success of key initiatives like the Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch (ROAS 6.6).
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising proved to be the most efficient channel, achieving the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 3.0% and Conversion Rate (CVR) of 2.8%. While CTV showed minimal direct conversion, it likely contributed to awareness.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Budget utilization was efficient across objectives, with Conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budget. The consistent week-over-week growth in conversions and revenue, peaking in Week 4, indicates improving campaign efficiency and stronger shopper intent over time.

### Campaigns Comparison
**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: The data suggests a strong alignment between campaign objectives and outcomes, particularly for conversion-focused campaigns which demonstrated high ROAS and efficient budget utilization.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: Overall budget utilization was efficient, with all objectives (Awareness, Conversion, Consideration) showing actual spend close to planned budgets (90-93% utilization). This indicates good financial management and pacing.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for direct conversions and engagement (high CTR/CVR), while CTV appears to play a role in broader awareness, as expected. This suggests a balanced channel strategy is in place.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: While CTV shows lower direct conversion metrics, its contribution to the overall brand awareness and potentially assisting conversions through other channels should be considered. The high ROAS of the "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign highlights the potential for specific product launches to drive exceptional returns.
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to invest in and optimize onsite experiences given their strong performance. Explore the full funnel impact of awareness-driven channels like CTV to understand their indirect contributions to sales.

## 6. Recommendations

## 1. Conduct Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis
**Action**: Analyze the factors contributing to the ROAS peak around July 15th (e.g., specific promotions, ad creatives, media placements, or external events) to replicate successful strategies.
**Objective**: Identify and leverage specific triggers or patterns that lead to higher conversion efficiency and ROAS.

## 2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization
**Action**: Based on the strong performance of Conversion campaigns and the high ROAS of specific campaigns (e.g., Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch), consider reallocating budget dynamically towards these high-performing areas.
**Objective**: Maximize overall campaign ROI by prioritizing investment in proven effective campaigns and objectives, while ensuring sufficient spend for awareness and consideration.

## 3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements
**Action**: Given the high efficiency of Onsite channels, conduct A/B tests on ad creatives, landing page designs, and call-to-actions within the onsite environment.
**Objective**: Further enhance CTR and CVR on the most effective channels to drive incremental conversions and revenue.
