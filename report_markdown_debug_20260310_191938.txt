# Campaign Performance Report for Dove Campaigns - July 2025

## 1. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive overview of the performance of Dove campaigns during July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and weekly revenue and conversion trends.

The top-performing campaign by ROAS was "CMP_2025_0001 (Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch)" with an impressive ROAS of 6.6, contributing significantly to the brand's total attributed sales. Overall daily ROAS for Dove campaigns remained strong throughout July, consistently above 5.5 and peaking at 7.0 around July 15th, indicating robust mid-month conversion efficiency.

Channel performance analysis revealed that Onsite ads achieved the highest engagement (CTR 3.0%) and conversion efficiency (CVR 2.8%), demonstrating their direct impact on sales. Offsite channels also performed well, while Connected TV (CTV) primarily contributed to awareness rather than direct conversions.

Budget utilization across campaign objectives was efficient, with Conversion campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budget, Awareness campaigns 93%, and Consideration campaigns 88%. This suggests a disciplined approach to spend management.

Weekly performance showed a positive trend, with conversions peaking in Week 4 and attributed revenue reaching $17.6K, indicating improving campaign efficiency and stronger shopper intent towards the end of the month. These insights are crucial for optimizing future marketing spend, resource allocation, and targeting strategies to maximize ROI.
