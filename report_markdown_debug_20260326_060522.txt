## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

Campaign CMP_2025_0011, targeting 'Consideration' for the Lux brand, ran from August 4, 2025, to September 5, 2025. It utilized 'Sponsored Product' media types primarily through the 'Onsite' channel. The campaign achieved 100% budget utilization, with both planned and actual spend totaling $25.83. The primary sub-objective was engagement and clicks.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

The campaign delivered 74,913 impressions, reaching 3,060 unique users. It generated 24 clicks, resulting in a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 0.032% and a Cost Per Click (CPC) of $1.076. The campaign's focus on 'Consideration' through 'Sponsored Product' on the 'Onsite' channel indicates an effort to drive user engagement and interest.

### 3.3. Channel and Format Performance

The 'Onsite' channel demonstrated a fluctuating but consistent ad spend and impression delivery throughout the campaign duration. Weekly ad spend peaked around the second week of August, while impressions were highest in the first two weeks before declining. This suggests an initial strong push in ad delivery, followed by a reduction in later weeks.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

The 100% budget utilization indicates efficient spending as per the plan. However, the relatively low CTR (0.032%) suggests potential areas for optimization in ad creative, targeting, or placement to enhance engagement and drive more clicks within the 'Consideration' objective. Further analysis into the weekly trends of spend and impressions can help identify optimal periods for ad delivery.
