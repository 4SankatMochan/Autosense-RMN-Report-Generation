**Executive Summary: Lifebuoy App Retention Campaign (CMP_2025_2158)**

The "CMP_2025_2158 - Lifebuoy - App - Retention - In-App Ads - 20250613-20250720" campaign for Lifebuoy, targeting retention with a sub-objective of subscription or refill, operated from June 13 to July 20, 2025. The campaign fully utilized its planned budget of $271.02.

Campaign spending demonstrated a consistent increase weekly, from $128.38 in Week 23 to $5242.10 in Week 28. Impressions also showed dynamic growth, peaking in Week 28 despite initial fluctuations.

Analysis of weekly performance indicates an initial ramp-up, followed by a period of strong performance in the middle weeks. During this optimal phase, the campaign achieved stable clicks and conversions (150-210 clicks, 21 conversions), attributed sales values between $950-$1,200, and a peak Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 1.15 in the week of June 29, 2025, suggesting profitability. Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) was significantly lower and stable during these middle weeks.

However, the campaign experienced a sharp decline in impressions, clicks, conversions, attributed sales value, and ROAS in the final week, coinciding with full budget utilization. This suggests potential premature cessation of activity during a profitable period.

Key recommendations include optimizing future budget allocations to ensure sustained performance, thoroughly investigating the end-of-campaign decline to identify contributing factors beyond budget, leveraging the successful strategies from the high-performing middle weeks, and improving campaign metadata tracking for enhanced future analysis.
