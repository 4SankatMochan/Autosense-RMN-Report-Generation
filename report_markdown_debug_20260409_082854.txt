## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report provides a detailed analysis of Campaign CMP_2025_0001 for the brand Dove, focusing on its performance from August 2nd to August 19th, 2025. The campaign's primary objective was 'Consideration' with a sub-objective of 'App Installs', executed through 'Sponsored Product' media type on the 'Onsite' channel. The insights aim to guide future marketing spend optimization, resource allocation, and targeting strategies for enhanced ROI.

### Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated consistent profitability with an average Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 8.75, fluctuating between 6.24 and 11.45. Daily attributed sales varied from 171.42 to 314.55, averaging 240.23. Conversions ranged from 2 to 5 daily, with an average conversion rate of 10.51%. The Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) averaged 9.48, showing an inverse relationship with conversions. Impressions and clicks fluctuated daily, with CTR averaging 1.05%.

### Channel Format Performance

The campaign operated exclusively on the 'Onsite' channel, utilizing 'Sponsored Product' media. For this channel, the total ad spend was 494.49, generating 96,550 impressions, reaching 3,876 unique users, and resulting in 546 clicks. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) for the 'Onsite' channel was 0.57%, with a Cost Per Click (CPC) of 0.91. Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) and Viewed Units were not available or zero due to no video completions.

### Optimization Insight

The campaign's fixed daily spend of 27.47 significantly influenced efficiency metrics, with CPA, ROAS, and sales value directly correlating with the number of conversions. Days with higher conversion rates (up to 15%) led to lower CPA (5.49) and better ROAS. Conversely, days with high impressions and clicks but low conversions indicated less qualified traffic or issues with the landing experience. Optimizing for conversion quality and potentially dynamic budget allocation could further enhance efficiency.

##### Trends
*   **Conversions**: The number of daily conversions ranged from 2 to 5, averaging around 3 per day.
*   **Revenue**: Daily sales varied significantly, ranging from 171.42 to 314.55, with an average of 240.23. Sales and ROAS generally follow the trend of conversions; days with higher conversions lead to higher attributed sales.
*   **Impressions**: Impressions varied considerably, from 1,339 to 9,431, with an average of 5,362. These metrics fluctuate significantly day-to-day without a clear upward or downward trend across the observed period.
*   **ROAS**: The campaign shows strong profitability with an average ROAS of 8.75. The ROAS fluctuated between 6.24 and 11.45, generally following the trend of conversions.
*   **CTR**: The CTR had an average of 1.05%, with values ranging from 0.9% to 1.72%. The CTR also fluctuates, with several days showing the same 0.9% value, potentially indicating a baseline or rounding for this metric on those days.

##### Anomalies
*   **Fixed Daily Spend**: The `actual_spend_to_date` is constant at 27.47 for every day. This indicates a fixed daily budget rather than an accumulated spend, which significantly influences the CPA calculation (CPA = Fixed Daily Spend / Conversions).
*   **High Conversion Rates/Low CPA**: August 3rd, 10th, 11th, and 14th stand out with a high conversion rate of 15% (except Aug 3rd with 13.89%), resulting in a low CPA of 5.49 (or 5.49) due to 5 conversions.
*   **Low Conversion Rates/High CPA**: August 2nd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 13th, 16th, 18th, and 19th consistently show a conversion rate of 8% (or close to it) and 2 conversions, leading to the highest CPA of 13.74.
*   **Lowest Impressions/Clicks**: August 12th recorded the lowest impressions (1339) and relatively low clicks (23), yet maintained a high conversion rate (15%) and 4 conversions, resulting in a good CPA of 6.87 and ROAS of 6.24. This suggests high efficiency on that day despite low volume.
*   **Highest Clicks with Average Conversions**: On August 18th, the campaign achieved the highest clicks (55) and impressions (9431) but only resulted in 2 conversions, leading to a high CPA of 13.74 and a low conversion rate of 8%. This suggests the traffic on this day was less qualified or the landing experience was less effective.

##### Overall Impact
Overall, the campaign demonstrates consistent profitability, but there's considerable daily variability in efficiency metrics (CPA, conversion rate, ROAS) driven primarily by the number of conversions achieved, given the constant daily spend. Days with higher conversion rates and higher numbers of conversions are the most efficient.

## 6. Recommendations

### Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis
*   **Insights**: The campaign exhibits significant daily variability in conversion rates, CPA, and ROAS, despite a fixed daily spend. Certain days show high efficiency even with lower impressions/clicks, while others yield high volume but low conversion quality.
*   **Action**: Conduct a deeper analysis into the daily fluctuations, correlating performance with external factors (e.g., day of week, time of day, competitor activity, specific ad creatives running, or landing page changes). Identify patterns that lead to high-efficiency days.
*   **Objective**: Optimize campaign scheduling and targeting to align with periods of higher user intent and conversion potential.
*   **Timeline**: Immediate (next reporting cycle).

### Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization
*   **Insights**: The fixed daily spend strategy limits the campaign's ability to capitalize on high-performing days and mitigate losses on low-performing days, directly impacting CPA and ROAS.
*   **Action**: Implement a dynamic budget allocation strategy that allows for higher spend on days or periods demonstrating higher conversion rates and lower CPA. Explore advanced bidding strategies that optimize for conversions rather than just clicks or impressions.
*   **Objective**: Improve overall campaign efficiency and maximize ROAS by intelligently adjusting spend based on real-time performance.
*   **Timeline**: Short-term (within 1-2 months).

### Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements
*   **Insights**: The anomaly on August 18th, where high clicks and impressions resulted in low conversions, suggests potential issues with ad relevance, traffic quality, or the landing page experience.
*   **Action**: Initiate A/B testing for ad creatives, ad copy, and landing page designs. Focus on improving the alignment between the ad message and the landing page experience, and ensure the landing page effectively guides users towards the 'App Installs' objective.
*   **Objective**: Enhance the quality of traffic driven to the landing page and improve the conversion funnel effectiveness.
*   **Timeline**: Ongoing.
