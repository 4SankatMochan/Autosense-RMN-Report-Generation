## 5. Campaign-Wise Analysis

### 5.1. Detailed Campaign Performance Analysis

**Campaign Name:** CMP_2025_2158 - Lifebuoy - App - Retention - In-App Ads - 20250613-20250720
**Campaign ID:** CMP_2025_2158
**Campaign Duration:** 2025-06-13 to 2025-07-20
**Campaign Objective:** Retention
**KPIs Analyzed:** Impressions, Clicks, Conversions, Attributed Sales Value, ROAS, CPC, CPA, CTR, CVR.

Here's an analysis of the campaign performance for 'CMP_2025_2158' (Brand: 'Lifebuoy', Objective: 'Retention') for June and July 2025:

The campaign showed growth in several key metrics from June to July, but also significant deviations in spend and some efficiency concerns.

**1. Significant Deviations from Planned Outcomes:**

*   **Actual Spend vs. Planned Spend:** This is the most prominent deviation. The campaign significantly overspent its planned budget in both months:
    *   **June:** Actual Spend ($385.13) was 42.10% over the Planned Spend ($271.02).
    *   **July:** Actual Spend ($427.93) was 57.90% over the Planned Spend ($271.02).
    *   The overspend increased from June to July, indicating a consistent trend of exceeding the allocated budget. This needs immediate attention to understand if the increased spend was intentional (e.g., to scale performance) or an uncontrolled budgetary issue.

**2. Key Successes:**

*   **Growth in Core Metrics:** Despite the budget overruns, the campaign delivered positive absolute growth across primary performance indicators:
    *   **Impressions:** Increased by 87,738 (from 273,438 to 361,176), indicating wider reach.
    *   **Clicks:** Increased by 51 (from 432 to 483), showing more engagement.
    *   **Conversions:** Increased by 6 (from 54 to 60), leading to more successful outcomes.
    *   **Attributed Sales Value:** Increased by $399.00 (from $2,790.93 to $3,189.93), signifying higher revenue generation.
*   **Improved ROAS:** The Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) improved from 7.25 in June to 7.45 in July. This indicates that despite the increased actual spend, the campaign became slightly more efficient at generating sales value for each dollar spent.
*   **Stable CPC and CPA:** The Cost Per Click (CPC) remained stable at $0.89, and the Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) remained stable at $7.13. This is a positive outcome, suggesting that the cost of acquiring a click or a conversion did not escalate with the increased spend and activity.

**3. Areas for Improvement:**

*   **Decreased Click-Through Rate (CTR):** The CTR decreased from 0.16% in June to 0.13% in July, a drop of 0.03%. While total clicks increased, the rate at which impressions converted into clicks declined. This could suggest:
    *   **Ad Fatigue:** The same ads might be shown to a similar audience, leading to decreased engagement.
    *   **Ad Placement/Targeting:** The increased impressions might be reaching less relevant audiences.
    *   **Ad Creative Effectiveness:** The ad copies or visuals might need optimization to maintain or improve engagement with a larger audience pool.
*   **Slightly Decreased Conversion Rate:** The conversion rate saw a minor decrease from 12.50% in June to 12.42% in July (a difference of 0.08%). While total conversions increased, this slight dip suggests that the quality of clicks or the landing page experience might need a review to ensure a higher percentage of visitors complete the desired action.

## 6. Conclusion and Recommendations

The 'Lifebuoy' Retention Campaign (CMP_2025_2158) has shown positive growth in key performance indicators such as impressions, clicks, conversions, and attributed sales value from June to July 2025, coupled with an improved ROAS and stable CPC/CPA. This indicates an effective campaign in terms of driving retention. However, the most critical issue is the significant and increasing deviation from the planned budget. Additionally, the decline in CTR and a slight dip in CVR warrant further investigation.

### Recommendations:

1.  **Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization:**
    *   **Action:** Investigate the reasons for the significant budget overruns in June and July. Determine if the increased spend was a strategic decision to scale performance or an uncontrolled budgetary issue. Implement a robust budget tracking and alert system. Consider dynamic budget allocation strategies that allow for scaling spend in high-performing periods while ensuring adherence to overall budget constraints.
    *   **Objective:** To ensure financial discipline and optimize resource allocation, preventing uncontrolled overspend while capitalizing on performance gains.

2.  **Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements:**
    *   **Action:** Conduct A/B tests on ad creatives, messaging, and audience targeting to address the decreasing CTR. Explore new creative variations to combat ad fatigue. Review the in-app ad placement and targeting parameters to ensure impressions reach the most relevant audience. Simultaneously, analyze the landing page experience and in-app conversion funnels to identify and rectify any friction points contributing to the slight decrease in CVR.
    *   **Objective:** To improve engagement efficiency (CTR) and conversion effectiveness (CVR) by continuously optimizing the user journey from impression to conversion.

3.  **Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis for ROAS:**
    *   **Action:** Collect more granular data on ROAS across different channels and over a longer time horizon. This will enable a more comprehensive analysis of ROAS trends and allow for channel-specific optimization strategies.
    *   **Objective:** To gain deeper insights into the profitability of different channels and time periods, allowing for more informed investment decisions to maximize overall campaign ROI.
