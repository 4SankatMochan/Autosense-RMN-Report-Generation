## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

This report details the performance of Lifebuoy's Retention campaign (CMP_2025_2158), which ran from June 13 to July 20, 2025, utilizing In-App Ads on the App channel. The campaign aimed to drive subscription or refill among existing users.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated strong retention metrics, achieving an overall Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) of 7.36 and a Conversion Rate (CVR) of 10.88% for the App channel, resulting in 114 conversions and 81 repeat transactions. The high budget utilization percentage indicates a significant investment relative to the planned budget.

### 3.3. Channel and Format Performance

The 'App' channel, leveraging 'In-App Ads', was the primary focus for this retention campaign. The performance metrics indicate that this channel is effective in driving conversions and repeat transactions for the specified objective.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

Analysis of monthly trends reveals mixed performance across different audience segments. While "Auto-Replenish Candidates" showed a decline in ROAS, "Brand Loyalists," "Churn Risk," and "Repeat Buyers" demonstrated improved ROAS, suggesting opportunities for segment-specific optimization. Click-Through Rate (CTR) generally remained stable around 0.5%, with "Repeat Buyers" and "Brand Loyalists" exhibiting slightly higher engagement. Tailoring strategies to these segment-specific insights can further enhance campaign efficiency and ROI.

*   **Action**: Investigate the specific factors contributing to the decline in ROAS for the "Auto-Replenish Candidates" segment between June and July 2025. *   **Objective**: To identify root causes for underperformance in critical audience segments and inform targeted adjustments to boost ROAS.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Action**: Reallocate a portion of the campaign budget towards segments demonstrating improving or consistently high ROAS, such as "Brand Loyalists," "Churn Risk," and "Repeat Buyers." Implement A/B testing for different bidding strategies tailored to these high-performing segments to maximize their potential.
*   **Objective**: To optimize overall campaign ROI by strategically shifting resources to capitalize on the most profitable audience segments.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Action**: Develop and execute A/B tests for various ad creatives and landing page experiences, specifically focusing on "Repeat Buyers" and "Brand Loyalists" given their higher CTR. Explore messaging, visual elements, and call-to-actions that resonate most with these highly engaged groups.
*   **Objective**: To further enhance engagement and conversion efficiency by refining campaign assets based on the preferences and behaviors of the most receptive audience segments.
