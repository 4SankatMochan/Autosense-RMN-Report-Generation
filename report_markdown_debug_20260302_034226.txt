## 3. Executive Summary

**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive overview of Dove's campaign performance during July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and conversion trends. The analysis highlights key areas of success and opportunities for optimization to maximize future marketing ROI.

*   **Overall Performance**: Dove campaigns demonstrated strong ROAS throughout July, consistently staying above 5.5 and peaking at 7.0 mid-month. The "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign (CMP_2025_0001) was the top performer with a ROAS of 6.6, significantly contributing to attributed sales. Weekly conversions and revenue showed consistent growth, with revenue reaching $17.6K in Week 4, indicating improving campaign efficiency over time.

*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising proved to be the most effective channel, achieving the highest Click-Through Rate (CTR) of 3.0% and Conversion Rate (CVR) of 2.8%. Offsite channels also performed well, while Connected TV (CTV) showed minimal direct conversion (CVR 0.2%), likely contributing more to awareness than direct sales.

*   **Optimization Insight**: Campaigns aimed at Conversion objectives utilized 90% of their planned budget, indicating efficient pacing. Awareness campaigns slightly underspent (93% utilization), and Consideration campaigns were more conservative (88% utilization). The strong performance of Onsite channels suggests further investment or optimization in this area could yield higher returns. The consistent growth in weekly revenue towards the end of the month suggests that sustained campaign efforts or retargeting initiatives were effective.

*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: Onsite channels exhibited strong mid-funnel engagement with high CTR and CVR, indicating that users interacting with onsite ads are highly motivated to convert. The lower CTR and CVR for Offsite and CTV channels suggest these may play a larger role in upper-funnel awareness and consideration.

## Campaigns Comparison

**Cross-Campaign Insights**

*   **Objective Alignment**: Campaigns like "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" (CMP_2025_0001) with a high ROAS (6.6) are clearly aligned with conversion objectives, driving significant returns. Campaigns with lower ROAS, such as "Dove Deodorant Awareness" (CMP_2025_0002) at 1.2, likely serve awareness or consideration objectives where direct ROAS is not the sole measure of success. The budget utilization by objective also shows a balanced spend across Awareness, Conversion, and Consideration, indicating a multi-faceted strategy.

*   **Budget Efficiency**: The overall budget utilization across objectives (90-93.3%) suggests effective budget management and pacing throughout July 2025. Conversion campaigns, which typically require more aggressive bidding, efficiently utilized 90% of their budget to drive sales.

*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for driving direct conversions and engagement (high CTR and CVR). Offsite channels provide a broader reach with moderate conversion efficiency. CTV, while having low direct conversion rates, likely plays a crucial role in upper-funnel awareness and brand building, aligning with the "Awareness" campaign objective.

*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There's a clear trade-off between reach/awareness-focused channels (like CTV) and direct conversion-focused channels (like Onsite). While CTV may not show immediate ROAS, its contribution to overall brand visibility and future conversions should be considered. The strong performance of the "Nourishing Body Wash Launch" campaign highlights the potential for high ROAS with well-targeted product launch efforts.

*   **Recommendation**: Continue to leverage Onsite channels for high-intent conversions. Evaluate the role of Offsite and CTV channels based on their contribution to overall campaign objectives, considering metrics beyond direct ROAS for awareness and consideration campaigns.
