## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

The Lifebuoy campaign 'CMP_2025_2158', focused on 'Retention' through 'In-App Ads' on the 'App' channel, demonstrated significant overspending with a spend utilization of 300% against its planned budget. While the campaign generated a substantial number of impressions and clicks, the absence of video completions (Viewed Units = 0) and consequently, an unavailable CPCV, indicates a potential issue with video ad engagement or tracking for this specific campaign on the 'App' channel.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

The campaign's total ad spend reached $813.06, significantly exceeding the planned spend of $271.02. For the 'App' channel, the campaign achieved 634,614 impressions and 2,950 unique reaches, resulting in 915 clicks. The Click-Through Rate (CTR) was 0.14%, with a Cost Per Click (CPC) of $0.89. A notable observation is the complete lack of 'Viewed Units' (video completions), which made the Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) metric unavailable.

### 3.3. Channel Format Performance

The campaign exclusively utilized the 'App' channel with 'In-App Ads' as the media type. Performance metrics for this channel show a high volume of impressions and clicks. However, the zero 'Viewed Units' suggests that the 'In-App Ads' either did not include video content, or if they did, the video completion tracking was not successful, or users did not complete viewing. This is a critical area for further investigation to understand the true engagement with the ad format.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

The 300% spend utilization highlights a significant deviation from the planned budget, requiring immediate attention to budget management and allocation strategies. The lack of 'Viewed Units' and 'CPCV' data for 'In-App Ads' on the 'App' channel indicates a gap in understanding video engagement. Future optimization efforts should focus on verifying video ad content, ensuring proper tracking of video completions, and reassessing budget allocation to align with campaign objectives and expected performance.

## 7. Recommendations

### 7.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Insights**: The campaign significantly overspent its budget (300% utilization), yet detailed daily performance trends for key metrics like unique reach, impressions, and ad spend were not available for the specified objective. The complete absence of video completions for 'In-App Ads' is a critical gap in understanding engagement.
*   **Action**: Conduct a detailed daily breakdown of actual spend versus planned spend. Investigate the reasons for the budget overrun. Implement enhanced tracking for 'Viewed Units' for 'In-App Ads' to accurately measure video completion rates. Analyze daily performance metrics (impressions, clicks, reach) to identify specific periods of high/low performance and corresponding spend.
*   **Objective**: To gain a clearer understanding of daily budget consumption patterns, identify potential overspending triggers, and accurately measure user engagement with 'In-App Ads' to ensure budget efficiency.
*   **Timeline**: Immediate (within 1 week) for budget analysis and tracking implementation; ongoing for performance monitoring.

### 7.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Insights**: The substantial overspending indicates a potential mismatch between the planned budget and the campaign's execution or bidding strategy. Without daily trend data, it's difficult to pinpoint when and why the budget was exceeded.
*   **Action**: Review and adjust the campaign's bidding strategy to ensure it aligns with the planned budget and retention objectives. Implement daily budget caps and alerts to prevent future overspending. Explore dynamic budget allocation models that can adjust spend based on real-time performance to optimize for retention.
*   **Objective**: To control campaign expenditure, prevent budget overruns, and ensure that ad spend is efficiently utilized to achieve the 'Retention' objective.
*   **Timeline**: Short-term (within 2 weeks) for strategy review and adjustment; continuous monitoring.

### 7.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Insights**: The 'App' channel's 'In-App Ads' showed 0 'Viewed Units', suggesting either non-video ad formats or a complete lack of video engagement/tracking. Even with impressions and clicks, the ultimate retention goal might not be met if the ad content isn't compelling or the post-click experience is suboptimal.
*   **Action**: If 'In-App Ads' include video, A/B test different video creatives and lengths to improve completion rates. If they are static, test various ad copies, calls-to-action, and visual elements. Ensure the landing experience within the app is seamless and directly supports the 'Subscription or Refill' sub-objective.
*   **Objective**: To enhance the effectiveness of 'In-App Ads' by improving creative engagement and optimizing the user journey post-click, ultimately driving higher retention rates.
*   **Timeline**: Mid-term (within 1 month) for planning and initial tests; ongoing for continuous optimization.
