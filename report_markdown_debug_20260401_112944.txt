## 2. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive overview of Campaign `CMP_2025_0005` for Kissan, which aimed for 'Consideration' (App Installs) through 'Sponsored Product' placements on the 'Onsite' channel. The campaign ran from July 29, 2025, to September 24, 2025.

### 2.1. Overview

The campaign exhibited a significant budget overutilization, with an actual spend of 5521.66 against a planned spend of 2760.83 (as per the overview table), resulting in a 200.00% spend utilization. Daily trends for key metrics such as ad spend, clicks, impressions, unique reach, and Click-Through Rate (CTR) were analyzed for the 'Onsite' channel.

### 2.2. Overall Performance

While daily engagement metrics like impressions and clicks show fluctuations, a critical finding is the absence of recorded sales data (`sum_of_units_sold` is null across all retrieved records) and zero video completions. The average CTR for the 'Onsite' channel ranged from approximately 0.9% to 2.5% during the observed period.

### 2.3. Channel/Format Performance

Performance was solely observed for the 'Onsite' channel with 'Sponsored Product' media type. Daily trends for ad spend, impressions, clicks, unique reach, CPC, and viewed units were tracked, indicating varying daily performance within this single channel.

### 2.4. Optimization Insight

The substantial overspend (200% utilization) combined with the lack of recorded sales or video completion data suggests a need for re-evaluation of budget allocation and clearer tracking of success metrics aligned with the 'Consideration' objective. Further analysis of daily trends can help identify optimal periods for engagement and cost efficiency.
