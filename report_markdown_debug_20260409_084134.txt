## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report details the performance of Lifebuoy's Campaign CMP_2025_2158, which had a 'Retention' objective. The campaign significantly exceeded its planned spend, showing a spend utilization of approximately 300%. Key performance indicators like Total Ad Spend, Conversions, CPA, and ROAS have been analyzed to provide insights into the campaign's effectiveness.

### Overall Performance

The campaign's total actual ad spend was 813.06, against a planned spend of 271.02, indicating a substantial investment. Conversions for the campaign were consistently at 3.0 daily, totaling 114 conversions for the 'App' channel. The Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) was 10.0, and the Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) ranged from 5.17 to 9.78, with an aggregated ROAS of 7.36 for the 'App' channel. Total Campaign Revenue for the 'App' channel was 5980.86.

### Channel Format Performance

For the 'App' channel, which was the focus of the aggregated metrics, the campaign achieved 114 conversions with a total ad spend of 813.06, leading to a CVA of 7.13 and a ROAS of 7.36. Total Campaign Revenue generated through this channel was 5980.86. This indicates a positive return on investment from the 'App' channel.

### Optimization Insight

The daily trend analysis revealed a consistent conversion rate of 3.0 throughout the campaign period (June 13, 2025, to July 20, 2025). This stability, while indicating consistent performance, also suggests a potential lack of responsiveness to changes in ad spend or other campaign parameters. Further investigation into the factors contributing to this consistent conversion rate, especially in light of the high spend utilization, could uncover opportunities for efficiency improvements or scaling strategies.

##### Anomalies

The campaign exhibited a significant spend utilization of 300%, meaning the actual spend was three times the planned spend. Despite this increased investment, the daily conversion rate remained constant at 3.0. This consistency in conversions, despite fluctuating ad spend, could be considered an anomaly requiring further investigation.

##### Overall Impact

The campaign successfully generated 114 conversions and 5980.86 in total campaign revenue through the 'App' channel, with a healthy ROAS of 7.36. However, the high spend utilization relative to a constant daily conversion rate suggests that the additional spend might not have proportionally increased conversions, implying potential inefficiencies in budget allocation or targeting.

## 6. Recommendations

*   **Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis**
    *   **Insights**: The daily conversion rate remained constant at 3.0 despite varying daily ad spend and a 300% spend utilization. This suggests that additional spend might not be translating into incremental conversions.
    *   **Action**: Conduct a deeper analysis into the correlation between daily ad spend fluctuations and conversion performance. Investigate if there are specific days or periods where increased spend yields diminishing returns or if conversions are capped due to external factors (e.g., market saturation, inventory limits).
    *   **Objective**: Optimize budget allocation for maximum conversion efficiency.
    *   **Timeline**: Immediate, for the next campaign planning cycle.

*   **Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization**
    *   **Insights**: With a consistent daily conversion rate, the current bidding strategy may not be effectively leveraging increased ad spend to drive higher conversion volumes. The ROAS range suggests variability in daily efficiency.
    *   **Action**: Implement a dynamic bidding strategy that adjusts bids based on real-time performance and conversion probability, rather than a fixed approach. Consider A/B testing different bidding strategies to identify the most effective one for driving incremental conversions within the 'App' channel.
    *   **Objective**: Improve ROAS and conversion volume by optimizing spend allocation.
    *   **Timeline**: Ongoing, with continuous monitoring and adjustment.

*   **Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements**
    *   **Insights**: A constant conversion rate could indicate that the current creative or landing page experience has reached its saturation point in terms of effectiveness.
    *   **Action**: Design and execute A/B tests for various creative elements (e.g., ad copy, visuals, call-to-action) and landing page optimizations (e.g., layout, content, user flow). The goal is to identify elements that can break the conversion plateau and drive higher engagement and conversion rates.
    *   **Objective**: Increase conversion rate and overall campaign effectiveness.
    *   **Timeline**: Next campaign cycle, with results informing future creative development.
