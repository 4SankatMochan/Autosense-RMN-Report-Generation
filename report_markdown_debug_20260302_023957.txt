## 3. Executive Summary
**Overview**: This report provides a comprehensive overview of Dove's campaign performance during July 2025, focusing on Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel effectiveness, budget utilization, and conversion trends. The analysis highlights key areas of strength and opportunities for optimization, crucial for the Marketing and Sales leadership team to inform strategic decisions for future marketing spend and resource allocation.

*   **Overall Performance**: Dove campaigns demonstrated strong ROAS throughout July 2025, with the top 5 campaigns contributing approximately 80% of total attributed sales. Daily ROAS remained consistently above 5.5, peaking at 7.0 mid-month, indicating robust conversion efficiency. Weekly conversions and revenue showed a positive growth trend, with significant increases towards the end of the month.
*   **Channel/Format Performance**: Onsite advertising channels proved to be the most effective in terms of both Click-Through Rate (CTR) at 3.0% and Conversion Rate (CVR) at 2.8%. Offsite channels also performed well, while CTV primarily contributed to awareness with minimal direct conversion.
*   **Optimization Insight**: Budget utilization was efficient across all campaign objectives, with Conversion and Consideration campaigns utilizing 90% of their planned budgets. The high ROAS of specific campaigns (e.g., Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch at 6.6) suggests opportunities for further investment and replication of successful strategies.
*   **Mid-Funnel Engagement**: Onsite channels show strong mid-funnel engagement with high CTR and CVR, indicating that users interacting with onsite ads are highly motivated to convert. The consistent weekly growth in conversions and revenue further supports effective engagement strategies throughout the campaign period.

### Campaigns Comparison
**Cross-Campaign Insights**
*   **Objective Alignment**: Campaigns generally aligned with their objectives, with Conversion campaigns showing strong budget utilization and high ROAS for top performers. Awareness campaigns also demonstrated efficient budget use, while CTV primarily served an awareness role.
*   **Budget Efficiency**: Overall budget utilization was high across all objectives (88-93%), indicating effective financial planning and pacing. The highest ROAS was observed in a product launch campaign, suggesting that specific, well-targeted product-focused initiatives can yield exceptional returns.
*   **Channel Strategy**: Onsite channels are highly effective for driving direct conversions and engagement, as evidenced by superior CTR and CVR. Offsite channels contribute significantly, while CTV plays a crucial role in brand awareness, even if direct conversions are lower.
*   **Performance Trade-offs**: There is a clear trade-off between awareness-focused channels (like CTV) and conversion-focused channels (like Onsite). While CTV has lower direct conversion metrics, its contribution to the overall funnel and brand visibility should not be underestimated.
*   **Recommendation**: Continue to leverage Onsite channels for high-intent users and direct conversions, while strategically utilizing Offsite and CTV for broader reach and awareness building. Focus on replicating the success factors of high-ROAS campaigns.

## 6. Recommendations

### 1. Conduct Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis
**Action**: Investigate the specific factors contributing to the daily ROAS peaks (e.g., mid-month around July 15th) and the consistent weekly growth in conversions and revenue, particularly in Week 4. Analyze concurrent promotional activities, media placements, or external events.
**Objective**: Identify optimal timing, contextual triggers, and campaign elements that drive peak performance to inform future campaign scheduling and content deployment strategies.

### 2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization
**Action**: Based on the high ROAS observed in campaigns like "Dove Nourishing Body Wash Launch" (ROAS 6.6) and the efficient budget utilization for Conversion objectives, reallocate a portion of the budget to similar high-performing campaigns or conversion-focused initiatives. Implement dynamic bidding strategies that respond to daily ROAS fluctuations to maximize returns.
**Objective**: Optimize overall ROAS and ensure maximum efficiency of marketing spend by shifting resources towards proven high-impact campaigns and objectives.

### 3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements
**Action**: Given the significant difference in CTR and CVR across channels, particularly the strong performance of Onsite channels, conduct systematic A/B tests on creative assets, ad copy, and landing page designs for each key channel. Focus initially on Onsite and Offsite channels to further enhance engagement and conversion rates.
**Objective**: Improve user engagement (CTR) and conversion efficiency (CVR) across all channels by identifying the most effective creative and user experience elements, thereby driving higher attributed sales.
