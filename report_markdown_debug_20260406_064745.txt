## 3. Executive Summary

### Overview

This report details the performance of Campaign 'CMP_2025_0003' for the brand 'Lux', which ran from August 1st, 2025, to September 11th, 2025, with a primary objective of Consideration. The analysis focuses on key performance indicators (KPIs), observed trends, and anomalies to inform future marketing strategies.

### Overall Performance

The campaign for Lux demonstrated strong profitability with Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) ranging from 6.44 to 11.83, despite a consistent daily planned spend of approximately 30.85. The actual spend significantly exceeded the planned spend. Click-Through Rate (CTR) varied between 0.9% and 2.5%, leading to 22 to 52 daily clicks. A robust Conversion Rate of 8% to 15% resulted in 2 to 6 daily conversions and Attributed Sales Value between 198.67 and 364.96. Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) ranged from 5.14 to 15. Impressions saw a broad range from 1,251 to 9,683, while Add to Cart Events were highly variable, from 64 to 845.

### Channel Format Performance

The campaign was executed solely through the 'Onsite' channel using 'Sponsored Product' media type. Daily trend analysis for ad spend, unique reach, and impressions for the 'Onsite' channel showed varying patterns. The ad spend for the 'Onsite' channel was $586.15 over the period, which aligns with the planned spend, but is significantly lower than the reported actual spend of $4957.0 for the overall campaign.

### Optimization Insight

A significant anomaly was the consistent zero performance across all video-related metrics, suggesting either no video ads were used or they were ineffective. New to Brand Conversions were extremely low (0 to 2), indicating the campaign primarily engaged existing customers. Despite consistent daily planned spend, other metrics showed considerable daily fluctuation. Future optimizations should investigate the discrepancy between planned and actual spend, explore strategies for new customer acquisition, and ensure all ad formats are effectively utilized.

## 6. Recommendations

### 6.1. Granular Temporal and Contextual Analysis

*   **Insights:** The campaign exhibited significant daily fluctuations in impressions, clicks, conversions, and sales value, despite consistent daily planned spend. There's also a large discrepancy between planned ($586.15) and actual spend ($4957.0) for the campaign.
*   **Action:** Conduct a detailed daily breakdown of actual spend versus planned spend, and correlate these daily spend patterns with the fluctuations in key performance indicators (KPIs) such as impressions, clicks, and conversions. Investigate the cause of the actual spend exceeding planned spend by a large margin.
*   **Objective:** To understand the drivers behind daily performance variability and the spend discrepancy, enabling more accurate budget forecasting and performance prediction.
*   **Timeline:** Immediate, within the next 1-2 weeks.

### 6.2. Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization

*   **Insights:** The campaign showed strong ROAS (6.44 to 11.83) and robust conversion rates (8% to 15%), indicating efficient spend when conversions occur. However, new-to-brand conversions were very low (0-2), suggesting a focus on existing customers.
*   **Action:** Implement a dynamic budget allocation strategy that allows for real-time adjustments based on daily performance trends and the identified optimal periods for engagement. Explore bidding strategies specifically aimed at new customer acquisition to increase new-to-brand conversions, potentially through audience segmentation or lookalike modeling.
*   **Objective:** To maximize campaign efficiency and profitability by aligning spend with performance, and to expand the customer base by acquiring new customers.
*   **Timeline:** Ongoing, with initial strategy adjustments within 3-4 weeks.

### 6.3. Systematic A/B Testing of Creative and Landing Page Elements

*   **Insights:** The campaign did not register any video-related metrics, suggesting either non-utilization or ineffectiveness of video ads. Add to Cart events were highly variable (64 to 845), indicating potential opportunities for optimization in the conversion funnel.
*   **Action:** If video ads are part of the intended strategy, conduct A/B tests on different video creatives and placements to determine their effectiveness. For the 'Onsite' channel, perform A/B tests on various elements of product pages and landing pages (e.g., calls-to-action, product descriptions, imagery) to optimize Add to Cart rates and overall conversion funnel performance.
*   **Objective:** To identify and implement high-performing creative and landing page elements that enhance user engagement and conversion rates, and to ensure all ad formats contribute positively to campaign goals.
*   **Timeline:** Commence A/B testing within 4-6 weeks, with continuous iteration based on results.
