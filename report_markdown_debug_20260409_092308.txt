## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

This report provides a detailed analysis of Lifebuoy's 'CMP_2025_2158' campaign, which ran from June 13 to July 20, 2025, with a primary objective of Retention. The campaign utilized In-App Ads within the App channel. Key performance metrics, including ad spend, conversions, revenue, and ROAS, have been evaluated to assess the campaign's effectiveness and identify areas for future optimization.

### 3.2. Overall Performance

The 'CMP_2025_2158' campaign demonstrated robust performance, particularly in its ability to generate revenue and maintain a strong Return on Ad Spend (ROAS). The total ad spend for the campaign was 813.06, significantly exceeding the planned spend of 271.02, resulting in a spend utilization of approximately 300%. Despite this substantial overspend, the campaign achieved a total campaign revenue ranging from 36.87 to 69.75 (on a daily basis) and an aggregated total campaign revenue of 5980.86. The ROAS ranged from 5.17 to 9.78, with an aggregated ROAS of 7.36, indicating efficient use of advertising dollars to drive returns. Conversions ranged from 1 to 1 (on a daily basis), with an aggregated 114 conversions, and CVA (Attributed Sales Value) ranged from 36.87 to 69.75.

### 3.3. Channel/Format Performance

The campaign was exclusively run through the 'App' channel using 'In-App Ads'. For this channel, the total ad spend was 813.06, yielding 114 conversions and a total campaign revenue of 5980.86. The Cost Per Acquisition (CVA) for the App channel was approximately 7.13, and the ROAS was 7.36. Metrics for Transactions Repeat, Units Sold, and Incremental Sales Lift were not available for this specific query.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

The campaign's high spend utilization (300%) coupled with a strong ROAS suggests that while the budget was significantly exceeded, the spend was effective in driving positive outcomes. This indicates a potential for more dynamic budget allocation strategies in future campaigns to capitalize on high-performing periods or channels more effectively, or a need for more accurate initial budget forecasting.

    *   **Objective**: Enhance campaign responsiveness and efficiency by identifying optimal spending periods and creative strategies.

2.  **Dynamic Budget Allocation and Bidding Strategy Optimization**
    *   **Insights**: The campaign significantly exceeded its planned spend by 300% while maintaining a strong ROAS (7.36). This indicates that the initial budget might have been too conservative or that the campaign found unexpected opportunities for efficient spend.
    *   **Action**: Review the initial budget planning process and implement more flexible or dynamic budget allocation strategies. Explore advanced bidding strategies that can automatically scale spend based on real-time performance to capitalize on high-performing periods without overshooting planned targets unnecessarily.
    *   **Objective**: Optimize budget utilization, maximize ROI, and improve forecasting accuracy for future campaigns.
    *   **Timeline**: Immediately for future campaign planning and execution.
