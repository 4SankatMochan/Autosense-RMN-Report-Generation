## 3. Executive Summary

### 3.1. Overview

This report provides a detailed analysis of the campaign 'CMP_2025_0001' for the brand 'Dove', focusing on its performance within the 'Consideration' objective. The analysis covers key performance indicators (KPIs) across various channels, weekly trends in ad spend, and overall performance metrics, including anomaly detection. ### 3.2. Overall Performance

The campaign demonstrated stable and consistent performance across the two weeks analyzed (Week 1 and Week 2 of 2023). No significant anomalies were detected in daily metrics such as impressions, unique reach, clicks, CTR, CPC, or CPCV, indicating that performance generally stayed within expected ranges. The total ad spend across all campaigns and days was $1745.0. On average, campaigns generated approximately 11,482 impressions and reached about 9,171 unique users daily, with an average daily click count of 232 and a Click-Through Rate (CTR) of approximately 2.34%. The average Cost Per Click (CPC) was about $0.39, and the average Cost Per Completed View (CPCV) was around $0.40, though the median CPCV was significantly lower ($0.06), suggesting potential skewness.

### 3.3. Channel Performance

Analysis of the 'Consideration' objective by channel reveals varied performance. Social media channels garnered the highest total ad spend and impressions, leading to the highest number of clicks and a strong CTR of 1.5%. Display advertising also contributed significantly to ad spend and impressions, with a CTR of 1.0%. Video advertising, while having a lower CTR (0.5%), incurred a substantially higher CPCV ($2500.00) compared to Display ($175.00) and Social ($83.33), indicating a potential area for optimization in video ad efficiency.

### 3.4. Optimization Insight

The consistent weekly performance, without significant anomalies, suggests a stable campaign. However, the varying performance across channels, particularly the high CPCV for video, indicates opportunities for optimization. Reallocating budget from less efficient channels or refining targeting and creative for video ads could enhance overall campaign effectiveness. Additionally, the stability in weekly metrics suggests that while performance is consistent, further testing and adjustments might be needed to drive significant improvements.
